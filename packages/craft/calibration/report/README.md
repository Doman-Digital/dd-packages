# Historical character reports: unvalidated drafts

All seven reports and retrofit checklists in this directory were generated
from the 2026-09-23 calibration. They are retained to reproduce those numbers;
they are not current visual reviews or approved client directions.

Phase G was reopened on 2026-09-27 after the user disputed MMM image evidence.
Its saved checklist also includes portal routes and planning-file findings
in a public-site review. Source matches require a rendered occurrence before
they justify public-site design changes. Old counts are not current findings.

Do not use these files, or the original seven decision PDFs in Linear, to sign
off a retrofit. Each client needs a replacement desktop/mobile visual scan,
checked asset provenance and customer evidence, annotated screenshots, and
client decisions with reasons. See ROADMAP.md for the completion criteria.

The original artifacts are preserved. An unused picture has been reported;
its precise identity and all dependent recommendations remain unverified.
