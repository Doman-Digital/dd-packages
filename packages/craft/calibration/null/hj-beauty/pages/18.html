Ingredient spotlight

Why Korean formulas actually work

K-beauty built its reputation on layering lightweight, fermented and naturally-derived actives rather than harsh, one-size-fits-all treatments. Here's what shows up again and again in our bestsellers:

  • Snail mucin — repairs, hydrates, and fades post-blemish marks
  • Centella asiatica (cica) — calms redness and strengthens the skin barrier
  • Propolis — antibacterial and glow-boosting for congested skin
  • Niacinamide — evens tone and refines the look of pores
  • Rice water & ferments — gently brightens without irritation

Start with these three steps

You don't need ten products on day one. Build up gradually from a solid base.

Double cleanse

Oil cleanser to dissolve SPF and makeup, followed by a gentle low-pH foam.

2

Treat & hydrate

A hydrating toner, then one targeted serum or ampoule for your main concern.

3

Seal & protect

Moisturiser to lock everything in, and SPF every single morning — no exceptions.
