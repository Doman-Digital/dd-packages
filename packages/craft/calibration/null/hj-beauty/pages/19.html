Why Harrisons Beauty

Started in a spare room in Leeds. Still run the same way.

Harrisons Beauty began when our founder couldn't find a UK shop that stocked her favourite Korean products without the wait, the customs charges, or the doubt over whether it was even genuine.

So we built one. Every brand we carry is sourced directly and held in our own stock — nothing drop-shipped, nothing sitting in a container for months.
