Our Story

Korean skincare, made simple for the UK

Harrisons Beauty was founded by two skincare obsessives who kept importing the same Korean products for friends and family. Rather than everyone dealing with slow overseas shipping and surprise customs charges, we now buy directly from Korean manufacturers, hold full stock in our own UK warehouse, and pack every order ourselves.

That means the price you see is the price you pay, your order arrives in days rather than weeks, and if something's not right, returns are as easy as popping to the Post Office.
