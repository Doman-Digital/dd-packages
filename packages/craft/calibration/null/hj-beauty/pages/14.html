Our story

Started in a spare room in Leeds. Still run the same way.

Harrisons Beauty began when our founder couldn't find a reliable UK source for the Korean products she'd discovered through friends abroad — everything meant weeks of waiting and unpredictable customs charges.

So we started buying directly from Korean brands and their official distributors, holding real stock here in the UK, and shipping orders out the same or next working day. No drop-shipping, no grey-market imports — just proper K-beauty, close to home.
