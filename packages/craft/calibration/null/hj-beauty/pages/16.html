Why we only sell Korean skincare

Korea's skincare industry moves faster and tests harder than almost anywhere else in the world. Formulas are reworked constantly, ingredient lists are refreshingly honest, and textures — from watery essences to bouncy gel creams — are designed to be layered without ever feeling heavy.

We started Harrisons Beauty because we were tired of paying import duty and waiting three weeks for products that should arrive in three days. Every item we sell is checked in and shelved in our own UK warehouse, so you get the same routine, minus the hassle.
