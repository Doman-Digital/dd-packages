Why buy K-Beauty from Harrisons?

We started Harrisons Beauty because we were tired of paying customs fees on our own K-Beauty orders and waiting three weeks for parcels stuck in transit. So we brought the stock here instead. Every product on our site sits in our own warehouse in Leeds, checked for authenticity and ready to ship the same day.

  • ✔ No customs charges. Because your order ships from within the UK, you'll never be hit with an unexpected import bill on delivery.
  • ✔ Fast, trackable delivery. Most orders arrive within 1–3 working days via Royal Mail or Evri.
  • ✔ Verified authentic. We buy directly from brands and their official Korean distributors — never grey-market resellers.
  • ✔ UK-based support. Real people, real skincare advice, based in Leeds and reachable by email or live chat.
