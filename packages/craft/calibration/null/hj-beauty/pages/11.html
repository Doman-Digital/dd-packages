Shipping & returns

How fast will my order arrive?

Orders placed before 2pm on a working day are dispatched the same day via Royal Mail Tracked 24 or Tracked 48. Most customers receive their order within 1–3 working days.

Will I be charged customs or import fees?

No. All stock is already in the UK, so there are no customs charges, import VAT, or handling fees — the price at checkout is final.

What's your returns policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. If something arrives damaged or faulty, contact us and we'll sort a replacement or refund straight away.
