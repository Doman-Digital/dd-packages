Why shop with us

K-beauty, without the wait or the worry

Harrisons Beauty was started with one frustration in mind: ordering Korean skincare from overseas sellers, then waiting weeks for it to clear customs — if it turned up at all.

We buy directly from Korean skincare brands and their UK distributors, hold full stock in our own warehouse, and ship every order from right here in the UK. That means fast, trackable delivery and no surprise import fees at the door.
