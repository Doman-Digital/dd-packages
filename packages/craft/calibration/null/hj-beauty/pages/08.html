Our story

Started in a spare room, now shipping across the UK

Harrisons Beauty began in 2021 when our founder, a longtime K-beauty enthusiast, got frustrated waiting three weeks for a parcel from Seoul — only to be charged £11 in customs fees on arrival.

So we brought the stock here instead. We now hold hundreds of Korean skincare products in our own UK warehouse, working directly with brands so you get genuine products at fair prices, without the wait.

We're still a small team, based in Leeds, who genuinely love this category — and it shows in how we pack, ship, and talk to our customers.
