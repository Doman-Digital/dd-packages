Our Story

Started in a spare bedroom in Kent

Harrisons Beauty began in 2021 when our founder, tired of six-week waits and unexpected customs bills, started importing her favourite Korean skincare in bulk and sharing it with friends. Word spread — and Harrisons Beauty was born.

Today we hold every product in our own UK warehouse, so nothing you order has to cross a border after you click "buy". Same great K-beauty, without the wait.

  • ✓ Every product held in UK stock — no import delays
  • ✓ Sourced directly from Korean brands and distributors
  • ✓ Never tested on animals
  • ✓ Real advice from a small, obsessive skincare team
