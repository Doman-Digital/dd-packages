Our Story

Started by two sisters, a spreadsheet, and a very full skincare shelf

Harrisons Beauty began in a spare bedroom in Manchester in 2021, when sisters Claire and Louise Harrison got tired of watching UK customers pay more in customs charges and shipping than the products themselves cost — or wait a month for a parcel that got stuck at the border.

So they started buying stock directly from Korean brands and distributors, importing it properly and in bulk, and holding it in a small unit just outside Manchester. Today that "small unit" is a proper warehouse, but the idea hasn't changed: buy it right, hold it here, and get it to your door in a day or two — not a month.

— Claire & Louise, Founders
