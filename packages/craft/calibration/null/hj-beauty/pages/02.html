Why we hold every product in the UK

Harrisons Beauty was started in Manchester after our founder spent years importing K-beauty from marketplaces abroad — waiting weeks, paying surprise customs fees, and sometimes receiving products that had sat unrefrigerated in transit.

So we built something different: a fully stocked UK warehouse, sourced directly from Korean manufacturers and distributors. Every bottle you order is already here, checked for authenticity and batch freshness, ready to post the same or next working day.
