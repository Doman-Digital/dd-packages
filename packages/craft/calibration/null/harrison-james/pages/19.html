FAQs

Common questions

Do I need to be based near Manchester to work with you?

Not at all. Around 70% of our clients work with us entirely remotely — by phone, video call and cloud software — from across England, Scotland and Wales.

How long does switching accountants take?

Typically 10–14 days. We handle professional clearance with your current accountant and manage the transfer of records, so it requires very little from you.

Are your fees really fixed, with no hidden extras?

Yes. We agree your fee upfront based on your specific circumstances, and it won't change unless your circumstances materially change — we'll always discuss that with you first.

Do you help with IR35 and contractor status?

Yes, this is one of our core specialisms. We review contracts, assess status, and advise on the most tax-efficient way to operate as a contractor under current legislation.

Can you help landlords decide whether to incorporate?

Yes. We model the tax impact of holding property personally versus through a limited company, factoring in Section 24, CGT, and stamp duty, so you can make an informed decision.

What software do you use?

We're certified partners for Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent, and we're happy to work with whichever platform suits you best — or recommend one if you're starting fresh.
