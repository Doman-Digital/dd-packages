FAQs

Common questions

Do you work with clients outside your local area? +

Yes — the majority of our clients are managed entirely remotely via video calls, phone and cloud accounting software, so location is never a barrier.

How long does it take to switch accountants? +

Most switches take 1–2 weeks. We contact your existing accountant directly for professional clearance and handle the transfer of records, so it requires minimal input from you.

Are your fees really fixed? +

Yes. Once agreed, your monthly fee is fixed and covers everything in scope — including reasonable phone and email support. We'll only requote if your circumstances materially change.

Can you help with historic tax problems or HMRC enquiries? +

Yes. We regularly support clients with HMRC enquiries, disclosures and correcting historic filing issues, and can act as your agent for all correspondence.
