FAQs

Common questions

How quickly can you take over from my current accountant?

Most switches take two to three weeks. We request professional clearance and your records directly from your previous accountant, and handle the HMRC and Companies House paperwork so there's no gap in compliance.

Do you work with clients outside your local area?

Yes — the majority of our clients work with us entirely remotely, via phone, video call and cloud accounting software. We have clients from Cornwall to Aberdeen.

Are your fees really fixed, with no surprises?

Yes. We agree your fee in writing before any work begins, based on the complexity of your affairs. If your circumstances change significantly, we'll always discuss any adjustment with you first.

Can you help if HMRC has already opened an enquiry?

Yes. We regularly represent clients in HMRC enquiries and disputes, whether or not we prepared the original return, and will handle correspondence directly with HMRC on your behalf.

What software do you use?

We're partners with Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent and will recommend whichever suits your business — all our packages include the software licence at no extra cost.
