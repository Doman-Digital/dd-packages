What we do

Full-service accountancy, without the jargon

Every engagement starts with understanding your goals. From there, we build a service around exactly what you need — no bloated packages, no surprise fees.

01 Annual accounts & Corporation Tax

Statutory accounts prepared and filed with Companies House and HMRC, with tax planning built in throughout the year, not just at year-end.

02 Self Assessment tax returns

For directors, contractors, landlords and sole traders — accurately prepared and submitted well ahead of the 31 January deadline.

03 Bookkeeping & VAT

Cloud-based bookkeeping through Xero or QuickBooks, with quarterly VAT returns handled and Making Tax Digital fully covered.

04 Payroll & auto-enrolment

Weekly or monthly payroll runs, payslips, P60s and P11Ds, plus pension auto-enrolment compliance for your team.

05 IR35 & contractor advice

Contract reviews, status assessments and take-home pay optimisation for contractors working inside or outside IR35.

06 Property & landlord tax

Advice on holding property personally or through a limited company, mortgage interest relief, and Capital Gains Tax planning.

07 Personal tax & wealth planning

Income tax, dividends, pensions and Inheritance Tax planning for individuals with more complex financial affairs.

08 Company formation

Setting up a new limited company, including share structure, registered office and HMRC/Companies House registration.

09 R&D tax credits

Identifying and claiming R&D tax relief for eligible SMEs investing in innovation, product development or process improvement.
