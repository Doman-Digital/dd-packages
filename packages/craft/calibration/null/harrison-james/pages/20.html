FAQs

Common questions before switching

How quickly can you take over from my current accountant?

Most clients are fully onboarded within two weeks. We request professional clearance and your records directly from your previous accountant, so you don't need to chase anything yourself.

Do your fees really stay fixed?

Yes. Your monthly fee is agreed before we start and covers everything listed in your package. If your business changes significantly — for example, you take on employees — we'll discuss any adjustment with you in advance, never after the fact.

Which accounting software do you support?

We work primarily with Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent. If you're not yet using cloud software, we'll set you up and provide training as part of onboarding.

Can you help with IR35 specifically?

Yes. We carry out contract-by-contract IR35 assessments for contractors and advise on the most tax-efficient way to operate, whether that's inside or outside IR35.

Do you work with clients outside the West Midlands?

Yes, the majority of our clients work with us entirely remotely by phone, email and video call. Office visits are available by appointment for those who prefer to meet in person.
