FAQs

Common questions

How much do you charge?
Our fees are fixed and quoted upfront based on your turnover and the services you need — typically from £75/month for sole traders and contractors, and from £150/month for limited companies. No hourly billing, ever.
Can you take over from my current accountant?
Yes. We request professional clearance and all necessary records directly from your existing accountant, so there's nothing for you to organise. Most switches complete within two to three weeks.
Do I need to use Xero or QuickBooks?
We recommend cloud accounting for real-time visibility and Making Tax Digital compliance, and we're certified partners for both platforms. If you use something else, we're happy to discuss it.
Do you work with clients outside London?
Yes — the majority of our clients work with us entirely remotely via phone, video call and cloud accounting software, wherever they're based in the UK.
