About The Firm

Independent, chartered, and built for how work has changed

Harrison James was founded on a simple idea: accounting advice should reflect how people actually earn money today — through limited companies, contracts, rental portfolios and multiple income streams, not just a single payslip.

We're a firm of chartered and certified accountants regulated by the ICAEW, working with clients across England, Scotland and Wales entirely through cloud accounting, video calls and the occasional in-person meeting when it's genuinely useful.
