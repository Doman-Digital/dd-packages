FAQs

Common questions

How much does it cost to switch accountants?

Switching is free and we handle the entire process for you, including contacting your previous accountant and transferring records from HMRC. Most clients are fully onboarded within two weeks.

Do you work with clients outside Leeds?

Yes. While our office is based in Leeds, the majority of our clients work with us entirely remotely via phone, video call and cloud accounting software. Location isn't a barrier to great service.

What software do you use?

We primarily work with Xero and QuickBooks, both certified cloud platforms that let you view your accounts, raise invoices and track cash flow in real time from any device.

Can you help if I'm just starting a business?

Absolutely. We regularly help first-time founders choose the right business structure, register with HMRC and Companies House, and set up bookkeeping systems from day one.

Do your fees include tax investigation cover?

Yes, all our packages include HMRC enquiry support as standard, so if you're ever investigated, we'll represent you at no extra cost.
