Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to switch accountants?

Nothing. We handle professional clearance with your existing accountant free of charge, and there's no gap in service — we simply pick up your filings from the next deadline.

Do you work with clients outside Yorkshire?

Yes. While our office is in Leeds, the majority of our clients work with us entirely online via video calls, email and cloud accounting software, wherever they're based in the UK.

Can you help if I'm already under an HMRC enquiry?

Yes, we regularly represent clients during HMRC enquiries and compliance checks, including Self Assessment, VAT and Corporation Tax investigations.

I earn over £100,000 — what should I be thinking about?

Above £100,000 your personal allowance tapers away, and above £50,000 with children you may face the High Income Child Benefit Charge. We model pension contributions and income structuring to manage both.

Which software do you use?

We're certified partners for Xero and QuickBooks, and use Dext for receipt capture. If you're already using different software, we can usually work with it or migrate you at no extra cost.
