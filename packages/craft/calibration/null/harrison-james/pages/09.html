What We Do

Accountancy and tax, done properly

From day-to-day bookkeeping to complex tax planning, we cover the full range of services growing businesses and busy individuals actually need.

Annual Accounts & Corporation Tax

Statutory accounts prepared and filed with Companies House and HMRC, plus corporation tax returns done right and on time.

Bookkeeping & VAT

Monthly bookkeeping on Xero or QuickBooks with VAT returns filed under Making Tax Digital, with no last-minute scramble.

Payroll & CIS

Weekly or monthly payroll, auto-enrolment pensions and CIS returns handled for construction sector clients.

Self-Assessment

Personal tax returns for directors, landlords and sole traders, prepared with an eye on every legitimate allowance.

Contractor Accounts & IR35

Limited company set-up, IR35 status reviews, dividend planning and umbrella-versus-limited comparisons that make sense for you.

Landlord & Property Tax

Rental accounts, Section 24 mortgage interest planning, SDLT queries and advice on incorporating a property portfolio.

Tax Planning & Advisory

Proactive planning around dividends, pensions, capital gains and extraction strategy for higher and additional rate taxpayers.

Company Formation & Structuring

New company set-up, share structuring and group reorganisations, with the paperwork filed correctly from day one.
