FAQs

Questions we hear a lot

Do you only work with clients near your office?

No. We're a cloud-based practice, so we work with clients across the whole of the UK using Xero, QuickBooks and video calls. Most of our onboarding and ongoing meetings happen remotely.

How much will it actually cost?

Every quote is based on the complexity of your affairs, not guesswork. After your free consultation, we send a fixed monthly fee in writing before any work begins — so you always know what to expect.

I already have an accountant. Is switching difficult?

We handle the entire process for you, including contacting your current accountant for professional clearance and gathering your records. Most clients are fully transitioned within two weeks.

Do you offer tax planning, or just annual compliance?

Both. Compliance (accounts, returns and filings) is the baseline. On top of that, we run annual tax planning reviews to flag reliefs, allowances and structuring opportunities before deadlines pass.

Which accounting software do you use?

We're certified partners for Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent. If you're already using one of these, we can take over directly; if not, we'll help you choose and set up the right platform.
