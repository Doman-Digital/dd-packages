FAQs

Common questions

How quickly can you take over from my current accountant?

Most switches complete within two to three weeks. We contact your existing accountant directly for a professional handover, request your records via HMRC's agent authorisation process, and keep you updated throughout — you don't need to chase anyone.

Do you work with clients outside Manchester?

Yes. The majority of our clients are managed entirely remotely via video call, phone and cloud accounting software. We have clients across England, Scotland and Wales.

Can you help with an IR35 review for a new contract?

Yes. We review contracts and working practices against current IR35 legislation, provide a written status assessment, and advise on the most tax-efficient structure for your circumstances.

I only have one buy-to-let property — is it worth using an accountant?

Often, yes. Our landlord package covers rental accounts, mortgage interest relief restrictions and allowable expenses, and many clients recover the annual fee several times over through claims they'd otherwise have missed.

What software do you use?

We're certified partners for Xero and QuickBooks and will recommend and set up the platform that best suits your business, at no extra cost within your monthly fee.
