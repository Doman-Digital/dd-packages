Frequently Asked Questions

\n

Common questions, answered

\n
\n
\n
\n How quickly can you take over from my current accountant?\n

Most switches are completed within 2–3 weeks. We contact your existing accountant directly to request professional clearance and transfer your records, so there's nothing for you to chase.

\n
\n
\n Do you work with clients outside your local area?\n

Yes — the majority of our clients work with us entirely remotely via phone, email and video call, supported by cloud accounting software. We have clients across England, Scotland and Wales.

\n
\n
\n Are your fees really fixed, with no surprises?\n

Yes. We agree your monthly fee upfront based on the services you need. If your requirements change, we'll always discuss and agree any adjustment with you before it takes effect.

\n
\n
\n Can you help with an IR35 investigation or HMRC enquiry?\n

Yes, we regularly support contractors and business owners through HMRC enquiries and IR35 reviews, including representation and correspondence handling.

\n
\n
\n What software do I need to use?\n

We work primarily with Xero and QuickBooks, and can set you up on either platform as part of onboarding. If you already use different software, we'll advise on the best way forward.
