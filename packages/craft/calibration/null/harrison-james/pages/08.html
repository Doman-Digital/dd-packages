FAQs

Common questions

How much does it cost to switch accountants?

There's no cost to switch. We handle the professional clearance process with your existing accountant and can usually have your records transferred within a week, with no gap in your compliance cover.

Do you work with clients outside Manchester?

Yes — the majority of our clients are supported remotely by phone, video call and our client portal. We work with SMEs, contractors and landlords across the whole of the UK.

Can you help if I've received an HMRC enquiry letter?

Yes. We regularly represent clients in HMRC enquiries and can correspond with HMRC directly on your behalf. If you're not yet a client, get in touch as soon as possible — response deadlines are often tight.

What software do I need to use?

We're partnered with Xero and QuickBooks and recommend both for real-time bookkeeping. If you use a different system or spreadsheets, we can usually still work with what you have.

Do you offer fee protection insurance?

Yes, we offer optional fee protection insurance that covers the cost of our time if HMRC opens an enquiry into your affairs, so an investigation doesn't come with an unexpected bill.
