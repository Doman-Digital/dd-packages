About the studio

A studio built around one client at a time

Chair and Blade was set up to do things differently — one barber, one chair, one appointment slot at a time. There's no rota of barbers to choose between and no chairs lined up in a row. It's just you, in for your slot, with the whole session given to getting your cut right.

Tucked away on St John Street, the studio takes its cues from the surrounding streets of St John's — exposed brick, warm brass fittings, low light, and a well-worn leather chair that's seen a few thousand haircuts.

Every appointment starts with a proper conversation about what you actually want, followed by a wash, the cut itself, and a finish with a hot towel and a straight razor line-up. No clock-watching, no back-to-back bookings stacked on top of yours.
