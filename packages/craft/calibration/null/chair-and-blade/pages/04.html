The Studio — St John Street
The Barber

Cutting hair on St John Street, one client at a time.

Chair and Blade isn't a shop with six chairs and a radio turned up too loud. It's a single-chair studio tucked into the quiet end of St John Street, where the only name in the appointment book each hour is yours.

I trained across Manchester's traditional barbershops before deciding the trade works better slower — one person, undivided attention, no handovers and no apprentices practising on your fade. If you sit in this chair, you get me and the time this cut actually needs.

That means proper consultations, sharper lines, and a studio that never feels like a waiting room.
