The Studio

A cut above the queue

Chair & Blade was built on a simple idea: a haircut is a conversation, not a conveyor belt. Every appointment is one-to-one — just you and your barber, for the whole visit, in a small studio designed to feel more like a front room than a shop floor.

Set on St John Street in Manchester's Northern Quarter-adjacent stretch, the studio takes bookings one at a time. That means no rushing, no handing you off to a junior, and no background noise of four other conversations happening at once — just your cut, done properly.

Whether you're after a sharp skin fade, a classic scissor cut, or a full beard reshape, you'll get the same barber and the same attention every time you sit down.

Private studio, single chair Every appointment is exclusively yours — no shared bookings, no waiting room.
