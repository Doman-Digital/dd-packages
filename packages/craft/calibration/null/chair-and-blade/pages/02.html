Book a Chair
St John Street, Manchester

One barber. One chair. Your full hour.

Chair and Blade is a one-to-one barber studio where every appointment is booked for one person at a time. No queue, no rush, no second chair — just careful cutting and proper conversation, if you're in the mood for it.

"Precision over volume, since day one."
The Barber

Built for one client at a time

Chair and Blade was set up on the simple idea that a haircut deserves undivided attention. There's no team of stylists working around each other, no double-booking to keep chairs full — just a single barber, a single client, and a slot in the diary that's entirely yours.

The studio sits on St John Street, in the quieter stretch of Manchester's Northern Quarter, in a small space fitted out with vintage barber tools, warm lighting and good music. It's not built to move people through quickly. It's built to get the cut right.

Specialising in skin fades, classic scissor cuts and beard sculpting, every appointment starts with an actual conversation about what you want — and finishes with a hot towel, because that's how it should end.

— Chair and Blade, St John Street
