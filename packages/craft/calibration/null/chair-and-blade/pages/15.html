Chair & Blade opened in 2021 with a simple idea: barbering works better when it isn't rushed.

There's no team here, no rota of juniors, no shop floor of six chairs running at once. It's one studio, one barber, and one client at a time. Every booking gets a full, unhurried hour — from the consultation and the cut through to the finish and the styling advice you'll actually use at home.

The studio sits above street level on St John Street, in the quiet creative quarter near Spinningfields — close enough to the city centre to fit around a working day, private enough that it never feels like one.

Clients tend to stay for years, not appointments. Many started with a single cut and now have a standing slot every four weeks.

Joel Ashworth FOUNDER & BARBER, CHAIR & BLADE
