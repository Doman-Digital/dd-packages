The Studio

Run by one barber, on purpose.

Chair and Blade sits in a converted ground-floor unit on St John Street, a few minutes' walk from Deansgate. There's a single chair, a single basin, and a single barber — Theo Marsh — who books every appointment himself and cuts every head of hair that comes through the door.

Theo trained in Manchester and spent nine years cutting in busy city-centre shops before opening Chair and Blade in 2021. The idea was simple: strip away everything that makes a haircut feel rushed. One client at a time, one appointment at a time, with the full hour if that's what it takes.

There's no walk-in trade and no second barber to hand you off to. If you book with Chair and Blade, you're booking time with Theo.

Why One-to-One

The whole appointment is about you, not the schedule.

There's no double-booking, no second client waiting behind the chair, and no handing you between staff.

01

No overlap

Only one appointment runs at a time. Theo isn't juggling two heads at once, so your slot stays your slot.

02

Same barber, always

Whoever cuts your hair the first time cuts it every time after. No re-explaining what you want to someone new.

03

Private by design

One chair means one client in the studio. Quiet, unhurried, and without an audience.
