The studio

Why there's only one chair

Chair and Blade opened on St John Street because Danny wanted to cut hair the way he'd been taught to, without the noise, the double-bookings, or six clippers running at once. There's one chair in the studio, and it's yours for the length of your appointment.

That means no handing you off partway through a fade, no rushing the last five minutes because the next client's already waiting, and no guessing which barber you'll get — it's always Danny, every time.

"I'd rather do one cut properly than three cuts quickly. That's the whole idea behind this place."

Trained in traditional barbering and modern fade work, Danny's spent over a decade in Manchester chairs before opening his own studio in 2021. Regulars come for the fades and skin work; most stay for the fact that it's quiet, unhurried, and exactly the same barber every visit.
