The Studio

Just one barber. That's the point.

Chair and Blade was built around a simple idea: a haircut should feel like it's just for you. There's only one chair here, which means no handing you off to whoever's free, no rushing between three clients at once. When you sit down, you have my full attention until you walk out the door.

I trained across Manchester's independent barbershops for over a decade before opening this space on St John Street — a small, quiet corner of the city where I could do things properly: precise fades, sharp beard work, and old-school hot towel shaves, all at a pace that respects the craft.

Every appointment is one-to-one, booked by time slot, so you're never waiting behind a walk-in queue.

— Tom, Chair and Blade
