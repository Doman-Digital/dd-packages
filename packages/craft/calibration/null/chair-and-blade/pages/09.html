The studio

Why a one-to-one barber studio

Chair and Blade was set up around a simple idea: a haircut is better when there's no queue, no handover between staff, and no clock running on someone else's schedule. Every appointment is booked for one person, with one barber, for the whole slot — from consultation through to the finish.

The studio sits on St John Street in the heart of Manchester's Northern Quarter-adjacent creative district, a short walk from Deansgate and Spinningfields. It's a small, deliberately quiet space — no music competing for conversation, no other chairs to work around.
