The Barber

Every client gets the whole chair

I'm Tom, and I've been cutting hair in Manchester for over twelve years. I spent most of that time in busy shops with three chairs running and a radio too loud to think — good training, but not the kind of place I wanted to keep working.

Chair and Blade is what I built instead: a small studio on St John Street with room for exactly one client at a time. No handing you off between apprentices, no ten-minute cut squeezed between walk-ins. When you're in the chair, you're the only thing I'm working on.

I specialise in tight fades, classic gentlemen's cuts, and beard shaping, with an old-school hot towel finish on every visit.

Tom Ashworth Owner & Barber, Chair and Blade
