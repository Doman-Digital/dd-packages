About the Studio

Barbering, slowed down.

Chair and Blade was set up on the simple idea that a haircut deserves your barber's undivided attention. There's no second chair, no second barber, and no appointments running in parallel — when you're in the chair, you're the only client in the room.

Behind the blade is Danny Kirk, a Manchester-trained barber with over twelve years behind the chair, the last three of them here on St John Street. Danny built the studio to hold onto what got lost when barbershops turned into volume businesses: proper consultations, unhurried finishing work, and a barber who actually remembers how you like your fade.

— Danny Kirk, Barber & Owner
