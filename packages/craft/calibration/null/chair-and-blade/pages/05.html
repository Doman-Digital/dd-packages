The Barber

A studio, not a shop floor.

Chair and Blade was set up on the belief that a proper haircut needs space — space to actually talk through what you want, space to take the time a clean fade or a sharp beard line deserves, and space that isn't shared with three other chairs going at once.

Working alone on St John Street, in the heart of Manchester's Northern Quarter, every client gets a full appointment slot with nobody else in the room. It means fewer cuts a day, but every single one gets the attention it should.
