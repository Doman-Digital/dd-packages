The Studio

A studio built around one seat.

Chair and Blade sits on St John Street, tucked into Manchester's old cotton-trade quarter, a short walk from Deansgate and Spinningfields. There's no bank of chairs, no shop floor of trainees — just one barber, one client, one appointment at a time.

That means the studio is booked out, not walked into. It means your cut gets the whole hour, not fifteen rushed minutes between the next two names on a list. It means the same barber remembers exactly how you like your fade, your parting, your beard line — because there's only ever been one barber, every time you've been in.

It's a small way of working, on purpose. Fewer clients, more care.
