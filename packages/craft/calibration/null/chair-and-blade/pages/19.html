The Barber

Every appointment is one-to-one, from start to finish

Chair and Blade was built around a simple idea: a haircut is worth doing properly. There's no second barber taking the next walk-in while you wait mid-cut, no other conversations competing for attention. When you sit down, the studio is yours.

Tucked just off St John Street, in one of Manchester's most walkable corners of the city centre, the studio takes one client at a time, by appointment only. That means you get the time your cut actually needs — usually 45 minutes to an hour — without ever feeling like you're being moved along.

It's a small setup on purpose. Fewer chairs, more care.

— Chair and Blade
