Not a barbershop. A studio.

\n

Most barbershops run four or five chairs at once, and you get whoever's free. Chair and Blade runs one — mine. Every appointment is booked individually, so you're never rushed, never bumped, and never cut by someone you didn't choose.

Every detail, unhurried

\n

Appointments are spaced out on purpose. That means time for a proper consultation before the clippers come out, a hot towel with your shave, and a finish that's checked twice before you're handed the mirror.

How a visit works

\n

Because it's one barber and one chair, the whole visit is built around you.

\n
\n
\n
\n
01
\n

You book a private slot

\n

Appointments are set individually by call or message — there's no walk-in queue, so your time is reserved and protected.

\n
\n
\n
02
\n

We talk before we cut

\n

A short consultation on what's grown out, what's grown in, and what you actually want this time — not just last time's cut repeated.

\n
\n
\n
03
\n

You leave, not the next client waits

\n

Because it's a single chair, nobody's hovering for their turn. You get the full appointment, checked and finished properly.

The barber behind the chair

\n

Chair and Blade is run by one barber, trained through a traditional apprenticeship and over a decade behind the chair before opening this studio on St John Street. The idea was simple: strip barbering back to what it's meant to be — one person's full attention on one client's hair.

\n

Every client's cut is logged after each visit — fade height, guard lengths, the lot — so returning is quick and consistent, without needing to explain it all again.

\n
— Chair and Blade
