The Barber

Jamie Ashworth

Chair and Blade started as a simple idea: a haircut should feel like it's just for you. After a decade cutting hair across busy Manchester shops, Jamie set up a single-chair studio on St John Street where every booking gets his complete attention — no gaps in service, no half-finished conversations, no rushing you out for the next name on the list.

Every cut is planned around your face shape, your hair, and how you actually style it day to day, not a five-minute template. Whether it's a sharp skin fade, a classic scissor cut, or a beard reshape, the studio only ever books one person at a time.

— Jamie
