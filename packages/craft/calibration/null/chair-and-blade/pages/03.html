The Barber

Twelve years behind the chair. Still just one chair.

Chair and Blade was built around a simple idea: a haircut is better when nobody's watching the clock. I trained on Northern Quarter shop floors before opening this studio on St John Street, where I cut, trim and shave one person at a time — no apprentices learning on you, no second client waiting in the seat beside you.

Every booking gets the full hour if it's needed: a proper consultation, a fade or crop cut to the millimetre, and a hot towel finish with the straight razor. If you've been chasing the same haircut across three different barbers for two years, come and get it cut properly once.

— Your Barber, Chair and Blade

The Studio

Why one barber, one chair works

01

No handoffs

The person who books your consultation is the person holding the blade. Nothing gets lost between a front desk and a chair.

02

Real appointment times

One client at a time means your 10:30 is actually at 10:30 — not somewhere behind two walk-ins who arrived first.

03

A record of your cut

Guard lengths, fade heights and beard shapes are noted after every visit, so returning is always exact, never a re-explanation.

04

Private, on purpose

A small, quiet space just off St John Street — good for a proper cut, and just as good for an hour of not being overheard.
