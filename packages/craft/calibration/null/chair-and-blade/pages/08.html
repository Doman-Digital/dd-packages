The Studio

A studio built around one appointment at a time

Chair and Blade sits on St John Street, in the quiet stretch between the Northern Quarter and the courts — a small studio with one chair and one barber, by design.

There's no rota of stylists and no double-booking. When you sit down, that hour belongs to you: your cut, your conversation (or your silence), your pace.

It's a slower, more deliberate way to get a haircut — and it shows in the finish.
