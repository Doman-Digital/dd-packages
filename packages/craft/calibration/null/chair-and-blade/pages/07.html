The Barber

Fifteen years behind the chair, now with just one of them.

Chair and Blade was set up on the simple idea that a haircut is better when it isn't rushed. After years of working in busy city-centre shops, I opened this studio on St John Street to do things differently — one client at a time, by appointment only, with the whole session given to a single person rather than split across three chairs at once.

There's no team to train, no chairs to fill, no one else's schedule to work around. Just me, my tools, and the time it actually takes to get a cut right — whether that's a sharp skin fade, a classic scissor cut, or a straight-razor beard trim.

If you want a haircut with someone else's full attention on you for the whole appointment, this is the place.
