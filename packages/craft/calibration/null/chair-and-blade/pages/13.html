Owner & Barber
The Barber

Cutting hair on St John Street since day one

Chair and Blade was built around a simple idea: a barber shop doesn't need six chairs to do great work — it needs one, run properly. I trained across Manchester's barbershops for over a decade before deciding the trade-off wasn't worth it anymore. Rushed appointments, three conversations happening at once, and a client who never quite gets your undivided attention.

Here, it's just you and me. Every booking is a fixed slot, every cut gets my full attention, and every client leaves knowing exactly what happened to their hair and why. It's barbering the way it used to be practised, on a quiet street two minutes from the city centre.

— Tom, founder of Chair and Blade

Why One-to-One

Fewer chairs. Better haircuts.

Most barbershops are built to keep three or four chairs busy at once. Chair and Blade is built around one.

01

No handovers

The barber who does your consultation is the one holding the clippers, and the one who'll remember exactly how you like it next time.

02

Fixed appointments

Your slot is yours. No double-booking, no "just five more minutes" while someone else is squeezed in beside you.

03

A private room

One room, one chair, no crowd of onlookers. It's a quieter, more personal way to get your hair cut.
