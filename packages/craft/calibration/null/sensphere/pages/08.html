Our Services

Assessment and therapy, tailored to each child

From a first assessment through to ongoing therapy and school liaison, we work in a way that fits around your family and your child's needs.

01

Occupational Therapy Assessment

A thorough, play-based assessment of your child's sensory processing, motor skills, self-care and daily functioning, using standardised tools alongside clinical observation.

02

Sensory Processing Support

Identifying sensory sensitivities or seeking behaviours and building a sensory diet and environment strategies to support regulation across settings.

03

1:1 Therapy Programmes

Individualised, goal-led therapy sessions in clinic, at home or in school, working on fine motor, gross motor, self-care and regulation skills.

04

Handwriting & Fine Motor Groups

Small-group and individual sessions to build pencil control, letter formation, scissor skills and hand strength.

05

School Observations & Liaison

On-site classroom observations, teacher consultation, and written recommendations that schools can put into practice straight away.

06

EHCP & SEND Report Writing

Detailed OT reports to support Education, Health and Care Plan applications, tribunal evidence and annual reviews.

07

Parent & Carer Coaching

Guided sessions to help you carry therapy strategies into daily routines — mealtimes, mornings, homework and beyond.

08

Staff Training & CPD

Practical training for teachers and teaching assistants on sensory processing, regulation strategies and inclusive classroom design.

Our Approach

How working with Sensphere works

A clear, unhurried process so you always know what happens next.

Initial Enquiry

Tell us about your child and what you're noticing. We'll arrange a free 15-minute call to check we're the right fit.

Assessment

A comprehensive OT assessment, drawing on standardised measures, observation, and conversations with you and, where relevant, school staff.

Feedback & Plan

We share findings in plain language, agree goals together, and set out a therapy or support plan with clear next steps.

Therapy & Review

Ongoing sessions in clinic, home or school, with regular reviews so the plan keeps pace with your child's progress.

For Schools & Settings

A therapy partner your SENCo can rely on

We work directly with schools and nurseries to support pupils with sensory and motor needs — from a single consultation to an ongoing, embedded partnership across the school year.

Discuss a School Partnership
  • ✓ Classroom and playground observations, with written recommendations SENCos and teachers can act on immediately.
