Who We Help

Support tailored to every child's world

We work with children and young people aged 3 to 18 who experience challenges with everyday skills, sensory processing or motor development.

Sensory Processing Differences

Over- or under-responsivity to sound, touch, movement or light that affects daily routines.

Autism & ADHD

Building regulation strategies, routines and independence skills that fit each child's profile.

Developmental Coordination Disorder

Support for gross and fine motor coordination, balance, and physical confidence.

Handwriting & Fine Motor Skills

Practical strategies for pencil grip, letter formation and classroom writing tasks.

What We Offer

Our services

From initial assessment through to ongoing therapy and review, every plan is built around your child's goals.

01

OT Assessments

Comprehensive occupational therapy assessments covering sensory processing, motor skills and functional independence, with a clear written report and recommendations.

02

1:1 Therapy Sessions

Individual therapy delivered at our clinic, in the home, or at school, focused on the goals that matter most to your child and family.

03

Sensory Integration Therapy

Play-based, sensory-rich sessions that help children regulate their sensory systems and build tolerance for everyday environments.

04

School-Based Support

Classroom observations, staff training and practical adjustments that help a child access learning alongside their peers.

05

Parent & Carer Coaching

Guidance and strategies for home routines such as mealtimes, dressing, sleep and managing sensory overload.

06

EHCP & SEND Reports

Detailed reports to support Education, Health and Care Plan applications and annual reviews.

About Sensphere

Founded on one simple belief: every child deserves to feel capable.

Sensphere was founded by a HCPC-registered paediatric occupational therapist with over a decade of experience supporting children and young people in clinics, schools and homes across the UK. We take a warm, play-based approach rooted in evidence, always working in close partnership with the people who know a child best: their family and the school staff around them.

Every assessment and therapy plan is individual. We don't believe in one-size-fits-all programmes, we believe in listening carefully, setting meaningful goals, and celebrating progress at whatever pace it comes.
