What We Offer

Our services

From first assessment through to ongoing therapy, every plan is built around the individual child.

01 Sensory Processing Assessment

A full assessment of how your child registers and responds to sensory input, using standardised tools and clinical observation, with a written report and practical strategies.

02 Fine & Gross Motor Skills

Support for handwriting, scissor skills, dressing, coordination and balance, built through play-based, motivating therapy sessions.

03 School Observations & Liaison

On-site visits to observe your child in the classroom or playground, followed by recommendations shared directly with teaching staff.

04 Individual Therapy Programmes

Ongoing one-to-one sessions, at home, in clinic, or at school, with clear goals and regular reviews with parents and carers.

05 Parent & Carer Coaching

Practical, everyday strategies you can use at home for mealtimes, sleep, transitions and self-care, tailored to your child's needs.

06 Staff Training & Workshops

Training for school and nursery staff on sensory processing, regulation strategies and creating supportive classroom environments.

Our Approach

Therapy that fits around real family life

Sensphere was founded to make paediatric occupational therapy more accessible, more collaborative, and more connected to the places children actually live and learn — home, school and the community.

Every child we see is different, so no two therapy plans look the same. We combine evidence-based assessment with genuine listening, so families feel heard and schools feel supported.

  • ✓ Therapists who explain findings in plain English, not jargon
  • ✓ Flexible appointments — evenings, weekends and school-day visits

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a referral from a GP or paediatrician?

No. Most of our families come to us directly. A referral isn't required, though we're happy to work alongside any existing referrals or reports you have.

Where do sessions take place?

Wherever works best for your child: at home, at our clinic, or on-site at nursery or school. Many families use a mix, depending on the goals we're working on.

How long does an assessment take, and what do we receive?

Assessments typically take 60–90 minutes, followed by a detailed written report within two weeks, including practical recommendations for home and school.

Can you support an EHCP application or tribunal?

Yes. Our reports are written in a format suitable for EHCP needs assessments and can be shared directly with your local authority or SENCO with your permission.

What areas of the UK do you cover?

We offer in-person visits across much of England, with online consultations and coaching available UK-wide. Get in touch with your postcode and we'll confirm availability.
