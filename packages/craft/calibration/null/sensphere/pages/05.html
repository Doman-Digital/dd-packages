Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to book an assessment?

No. Families can self-refer directly for a private assessment. A GP or school referral isn't required, though we're happy to liaise with other professionals already involved.

How long does an assessment take, and when will we get the report?

Assessments typically last 90 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the child's needs. Written reports are usually delivered within 3 weeks.

Can you assess or provide therapy within a school setting?

Yes. Many of our assessments and therapy sessions take place in school, with parental consent, so we can observe your child in their everyday environment.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in the North West and see families across Greater Manchester, Yorkshire, and the wider North West in person, with remote consultations available across the rest of the UK.

Do you support EHCP applications?

Yes. Our assessment reports are written to a standard suitable for inclusion in EHCP applications and reviews, and we can attend annual review meetings where needed.
