About Sensphere

Understanding every child's world, one sense at a time

Every child experiences the world differently — how they move, focus, regulate, and respond to sound, touch and space all shape how they learn and play. At Sensphere, our occupational therapists look at the whole child: their sensory processing, motor skills, self-regulation and daily routines, so we can understand what's really getting in the way and what will help.

We work in partnership with parents, carers, teachers and other professionals to turn assessment findings into practical, achievable goals — whether that's handwriting, getting dressed independently, sitting at the table for dinner, or managing a busy classroom.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a GP or specialist referral?

No — for private assessments and therapy, parents and carers can refer directly. Schools and other professionals can also refer with parental consent.

Can your reports be used for an EHCP application or annual review?

Yes. Our assessment reports are written in a format suitable for supporting Education, Health and Care Plan applications, reviews and tribunal evidence.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer sessions in our clinic, at your child's school, at home, or online for review and coaching sessions where appropriate.

What areas of the UK do you cover?

We work with families and schools across England, with in-person visits available in most regions and remote consultations offered UK-wide.

How long does an assessment take?

A typical initial assessment takes 60–90 minutes, with additional time for standardised testing where needed. You'll receive a written report within two to three weeks.
