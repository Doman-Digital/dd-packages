Our Services

Every child is different, so every plan is too. We offer assessment and therapy services tailored to the individual, delivered wherever they'll have the most impact.

OT Assessments

Comprehensive assessments covering sensory processing, motor skills, self-care and daily living skills, resulting in a clear, actionable report.

Sensory Integration Therapy

Structured, play-based therapy to help children process and respond to sensory information, reducing overwhelm and building regulation skills.

Fine Motor & Handwriting

Individual and small-group programmes to build pencil control, hand strength and handwriting fluency at a pace that suits the child.

Gross Motor & Coordination

Support for balance, coordination and movement planning, helping children take part confidently in PE, playtime and everyday activity.

Independence & Self-Care

Practical support with dressing, feeding, toileting and other daily routines, building the skills and confidence for greater independence.

Parent & Carer Coaching

Guidance and strategies for the adults around the child, so therapy goals translate into calmer, more manageable daily routines at home.

About Sensphere

Sensphere was founded to make high-quality occupational therapy more accessible to children, young people and the families and schools who support them. We believe therapy should fit around a child's real life, not the other way round.

Our team consists of experienced, HCPC-registered occupational therapists with specialist training in paediatric practice, sensory integration and working within the SEND framework. We take the time to understand each child as an individual, and we communicate clearly with everyone involved in their care.

  • HCPC-registered occupational therapists
  • Enhanced DBS-checked and fully insured
  • Experience supporting EHCP and SEND assessments
  • Flexible visits: home, school, clinic or online

Frequently Asked Questions

A few of the questions we're asked most often. Get in touch if you'd like to know more.

Do we need a diagnosis before referring?

No. You don't need a diagnosis to access an occupational therapy assessment. Many families come to us simply because they have concerns about their child's motor skills, sensory processing or daily routines.

Where do assessments and sessions take place?

We offer flexibility around home, school and clinic settings, as well as online consultations where appropriate. We'll agree the best setting with you when we discuss the referral.

Can Sensphere support an EHCP application?

Yes. Our assessment reports are written to a standard suitable for supporting EHC needs assessments and annual reviews, and we're happy to liaise with the local authority or school where needed.

How long does an assessment take?

A typical assessment session lasts around 60-90 minutes, followed by a written report within two to three weeks. Timescales may vary depending on the complexity of the referral.

Do you accept self-referrals from parents?

Yes, families can refer directly, as can schools, GPs, paediatricians and other professionals involved in a child's care.

Ready to talk about your child's needs?

Whether you're a parent, carer or school professional, we're happy to have a no-obligation conversation about how we can help.
