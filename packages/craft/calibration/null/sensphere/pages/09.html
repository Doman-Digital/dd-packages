What We Offer

Assessment and therapy, tailored to every child

Every child is unique, so every plan we build is too. Our services combine rigorous clinical assessment with warm, playful therapy that children genuinely look forward to.

OT Assessments

Comprehensive, evidence-based assessments to understand how a child processes the world and moves through it.

  • Sensory processing assessment
  • Developmental coordination (DCD/dyspraxia) assessment
  • Fine and gross motor skills assessment
  • Reports for EHCNA and SEND tribunal

1:1 Therapy

Individual therapy sessions designed around each child's goals, delivered in clinic, at home or in school.

  • Sensory integration therapy
  • Handwriting and fine motor skills
  • Self-regulation and emotional literacy
  • Independence and daily living skills

Family Support

Parents and carers are central to every plan, with practical strategies you can use straight away at home.

  • Parent coaching sessions
  • Home-based sensory strategies
  • Sibling and family workshops
  • Ongoing progress reviews

School Partnership

We work alongside teachers and SENCOs to embed strategies into the classroom, not just the therapy room.

  • Classroom observations
  • Staff training and INSET sessions
  • Sensory circuits and movement breaks
  • Individual education plan input
Who We Support

Working alongside the people who know each child best

Progress happens fastest when everyone around a child is working together. That's why our approach is built around three groups.

Children & Young People

From early years through to secondary school, we support children with sensory processing differences, autism, ADHD, dyspraxia and coordination difficulties.

Families & Carers

We listen first. Every plan reflects what matters most to your family, with clear, jargon-free guidance you can put into practice at home.

Schools & Settings

We collaborate with SENCOs and teaching staff to make recommendations practical and sustainable within the realities of a busy classroom.

How It Works

A simple, supportive process from first enquiry to progress review

We know reaching out can feel like a big step. Here's exactly what to expect when you get in touch with Sensphere.

1

Referral & Enquiry

Get in touch via our form, phone or email. We'll ask a few questions to understand your child's needs.

About Sensphere

Founded on the belief that every child deserves to feel understood

Sensphere was founded by a specialist paediatric occupational therapist who spent years working across NHS, private and school settings and saw the same gap again and again: families waiting months for assessments, and schools left without the practical support they needed to help a child in front of them right now.

We built Sensphere to close that gap; combining clinical rigour with a genuinely warm, child-led approach, and making sure that every report and every session translates into something families and teachers can actually use.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a diagnosis before contacting Sensphere?

No. Many families come to us before any formal diagnosis, simply because they've noticed their child is finding certain things difficult. Our assessment can help identify what's going on and what support would help.

Can we use your reports for an EHCP application?

Yes. Our assessment reports are written to a standard suitable for EHC needs assessments (EHCNA) and, where needed, SEND tribunal proceedings.

Do you work directly with schools?

Absolutely. We regularly carry out classroom observations, deliver staff training, and help schools embed sensory and motor skills strategies into everyday routines, working alongside SENCOs and class teachers.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer sessions in our clinic, at your child's school, in your home, or online, depending on what best suits your child and family.

How long is the waiting list?

Waiting times vary by location and time of year. We'll always give you an honest, up-to-date estimate when you first get in touch.
