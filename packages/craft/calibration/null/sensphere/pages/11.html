About Sensphere

Child-led therapy, rooted in evidence

Founded by a team of specialist paediatric occupational therapists, Sensphere exists to help children who are struggling with everyday tasks — from getting dressed and using cutlery, to sitting still at a desk or coping with a noisy classroom. We take time to understand each child as an individual, and we work in partnership with parents, carers and teachers so that progress made in a session carries through into daily life.

  • Fully qualified, HCPC-registered occupational therapists with specialist paediatric training
  • Assessments and reports suitable for EHCP applications and school support plans
  • Flexible appointments at our clinic, in the family home, or on-site at school
  • Ongoing parent and teacher coaching, not just one-off sessions
What We Offer

Occupational therapy services for children and young people

From initial assessment through to long-term therapy planning, our services are built around your child's needs and goals.

OT Assessments

Comprehensive, standardised assessments of motor skills, sensory processing and daily living skills, with a detailed written report and clear recommendations.

1:1 Therapy Sessions

Regular, goal-focused sessions delivered in clinic or at home, targeting handwriting, coordination, self-care and independence skills.

Sensory Integration Therapy

Structured, play-based programmes to help children who are over- or under-sensitive to sound, touch, movement or visual input.

School-Based Support

On-site observations, staff training and classroom strategies to help children access learning and thrive alongside their peers.

Parent & Carer Coaching

Practical strategies and home programmes so families can build therapy techniques into everyday routines with confidence.

EHCP & SEND Reports

Independent occupational therapy reports written to support Education, Health and Care Plan applications and annual reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from families

Do we need a diagnosis before contacting Sensphere?

No. Many of the families we work with are still exploring what's going on for their child, or are on a waiting list for diagnosis elsewhere. An occupational therapy assessment looks at your child's functional skills and needs, regardless of diagnosis.

Where do appointments take place?

We offer appointments at our clinic, in your home, or on-site at your child's school, depending on what's most appropriate for the assessment or therapy goals. Some review sessions can also be held remotely by video call.

How long does an assessment take, and when will we get the report?

A full occupational therapy assessment typically takes 60–90 minutes. You'll receive a written report with findings and recommendations within three weeks.

Can Sensphere reports be used for EHCP applications?

Yes. Our assessment reports are written to a standard suitable for supporting Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) applications, annual reviews and school funding requests.

Do you work with schools directly, or only with families?

Both. Schools can commission observations, staff training or whole-class programmes directly, and we're happy to liaise with your child's school as part of an individual therapy plan, with parental consent.

What age range do you work with?

We work with children and young people from age 2 through to 18, adapting our approach for early years, primary and secondary-aged pupils.
