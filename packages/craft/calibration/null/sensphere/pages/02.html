Who We Support

A team approach, built around the child

We work directly with the three people who matter most in a child's daily life — no referral wrangling, no waiting lists that stretch into years.

Children & Young People

From toddlers to teens, we support development in fine and gross motor skills, sensory processing, self-regulation and daily living tasks like dressing, handwriting and mealtimes.

Families

Parents and carers are part of every step. We offer practical home strategies, coaching sessions and honest guidance — so support doesn't stop when the appointment ends.

Schools

About Sensphere

Founded by an occupational therapist who wanted something better for families

Sensphere was founded by Chartered Occupational Therapist Priya Anand, after years working in NHS paediatric services and watching families wait far too long for far too little contact time. Sensphere was built to close that gap: fast access, longer appointments, and real relationships between therapist, child, family and school.

Our small team now includes specialists in sensory integration, handwriting and school-based practice, all HCPC registered and DBS checked, all committed to the same thing — practical, honest, child-led therapy.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a referral from our GP or paediatrician?

No. Sensphere accepts self-referrals directly from parents and carers, as well as referrals from schools, GPs and other professionals. You can get in touch and book a free consultation without any paperwork first.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer appointments at our clinic in Leeds, in your child's school (with school agreement), or in your own home. Many families use a mix, depending on the goals we're working on.

Can Sensphere write a report for an EHCP or tribunal?

Yes. We regularly write independent OT reports for EHC needs assessments, annual reviews and SEND tribunal proceedings, based on formal assessment and observation.

What age range do you work with?

We support children and young people from age 2 up to 18, including transition-age young people preparing for college or further education.

How much does an assessment cost?

Costs depend on the type and setting of assessment. We'll give you a clear, written quote after your free phone consultation, with no obligation to proceed.

Do you offer support for schools directly?

Yes. Schools can commission Sensphere for pupil-specific consultations, staff training days, or whole-setting sensory audits. Get in touch and we'll put together a proposal.

Ready to talk about your child?

Book a free, no-obligation phone consultation and we'll help you understand whether occupational therapy is the right next step.
