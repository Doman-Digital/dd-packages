Assessment and therapy, built around your child

OT Assessments

Comprehensive assessments covering sensory processing, motor skills, self-care, emotional regulation and school participation, with a clear written report and practical recommendations.

1:1 Therapy Programmes

Ongoing therapy blocks tailored to your child's goals — from handwriting and fine motor skills to sensory regulation, using play-based, evidence-informed approaches.

School Consultation

We work directly with class teachers and SENCOs, observing children in the classroom and co-designing strategies that fit into the real school day.

Small-group sensory and motor skills sessions designed for schools and nurseries, plus staff training so strategies continue between our visits.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we're asked most often.

Do we need a referral to book an assessment?

No. Most of our families self-refer, though we're happy to liaise with your GP, paediatrician, or school if that's helpful.

Which areas do you cover?

We offer home and school visits across most of England and Wales, and video consultations for families further afield. Get in touch and we'll confirm availability in your area.

How long does an assessment take?

A full OT assessment typically takes 1.5–2 hours, plus time to review information from parents and school. Your written report usually follows within two to three weeks.

Can Sensphere reports be used for EHCP applications?

Yes. Our reports are written to a standard suitable for EHCP needs assessments, annual reviews, and tribunal evidence, and we're experienced in supporting families through this process.

Do you offer sessions in schools during the school day?

Yes, this is one of our most requested services. We work with schools to schedule visits, group sessions, and staff training with minimal disruption to the timetable.
