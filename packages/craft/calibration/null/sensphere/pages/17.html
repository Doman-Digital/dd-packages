👧

👨‍👩‍👧

🏫

What we offer

Assessment and therapy, tailored to the child

We combine standardised assessment tools with close observation of the child in their real environments, then build a plan around what actually matters to their day-to-day life.

01

OT Assessments

A full picture of a child's sensory processing, motor skills, and functional abilities, with a clear written report.

  • Sensory processing assessments
  • Fine and gross motor skills screening
  • Developmental coordination disorder (DCD) assessment
  • Reports to support EHCP applications
02

Sensory Integration Therapy

Play-based, one-to-one therapy sessions that help children regulate their sensory systems and build tolerance for everyday situations.

  • Individualised sensory diets
  • Regulation and self-calming strategies
  • Sessions in clinic or at home
03

Fine Motor & Handwriting

Targeted intervention for children who struggle with pencil grip, letter formation, scissor skills, or dressing and self-care tasks.

  • Handwriting and pre-writing programmes
  • Self-care and independence skills
  • Strength and coordination activities
04

Parent & School Coaching

Practical guidance for the adults around the child, so strategies are consistent and sustainable beyond the therapy room.

  • Home strategy sessions for parents
  • Staff training and classroom advice
  • Ongoing review and progress reports
Our process

Simple, four-step pathway

From first enquiry to ongoing support, we keep families informed at every stage.

01

Initial enquiry

Tell us about your child and what's prompted you to reach out. We'll arrange a short call to check we're a good fit.

02

Assessment

A thorough assessment session, drawing on standardised tools, observation, and conversations with parents and, where relevant, school staff.

03

Report & plan

You'll receive a clear written report with findings and a personalised therapy or support plan.

04

Ongoing therapy

Regular sessions with periodic reviews, adjusting the plan as your child grows and progresses.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a diagnosis before contacting Sensphere?

No. Many families come to us before, during, or without ever seeking a formal diagnosis. Our assessments look at how your child functions day to day, regardless of diagnostic status.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer assessments and therapy in our clinic, in your child's school (with the setting's agreement), or in your home, depending on what suits your child best.

How long does an assessment take?

A typical assessment session lasts around 90 minutes to two hours, followed by a written report within two to three weeks.

Can you provide reports for EHCP applications?

Yes. Our assessment reports are commonly used to support Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) applications and annual reviews.

Do you accept referrals from schools or other professionals?

Yes, alongside direct enquiries from parents and carers, we welcome referrals from schools, GPs, paediatricians and other allied health professionals.

What areas do you cover?

We offer in-person visits across the UK depending on location, and can arrange online consultations for parent coaching and school liaison where a home visit isn't practical.
