Our Approach

Therapy that fits into real family life, not the other way round

Sensphere was founded on a simple belief: children make the most progress when therapy happens in the places and routines that matter to them — not in isolation from home and school.

Our team of qualified, HCPC-registered occupational therapists works collaboratively with parents, carers, teachers and other professionals, so every recommendation is realistic, specific, and actually gets used.

  • Child-led, play-based sessions grounded in evidence
  • Close collaboration with schools, SENCOs and GPs
  • Clear, jargon-free reports you can actually use
  • Flexible delivery: in clinic, at home, or in school

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral from a GP or paediatrician?

No — you can self-refer directly to Sensphere. Many of our families come to us without a formal referral, though we're happy to work alongside any professionals already involved.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer assessments and therapy at our clinic, in your home, or at your child's school, depending on what suits your family and your child's goals best.

Can your reports be used for an EHCP or tribunal?

Yes. Our assessment reports are written to a standard suitable for EHCP needs assessments, annual reviews, and tribunal evidence, and our therapists have experience giving evidence when required.

How long is the waiting list?

Waiting times vary by location and service, but we aim to offer an initial consultation within two to three weeks. Get in touch and we'll give you the most current estimate.

Do you accept funding through Direct Payments or Personal Budgets?

Yes, we regularly work with families using Direct Payments, Personal Health Budgets, and Local Authority funding. Get in touch and we'll talk you through what's needed.

What areas of the UK do you cover?

Our therapists visit families and schools across England, with clinic bases in several major cities. We also offer selected assessments and consultations remotely — ask us about availability in your area.
