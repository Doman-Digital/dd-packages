Our Approach

Child-led, evidence-based, family-focused

At Sensphere, therapy never feels like "work". We use a play-based, child-led approach grounded in the latest occupational therapy research, so children build genuine skills while doing what they naturally love — playing, exploring and moving.

  • ✓ Sessions designed around your child's interests, not a rigid curriculum
  • ✓ Clear, measurable goals agreed together with families
  • ✓ Strategies that translate into real classroom and home routines
  • ✓ Regular, honest communication — no jargon, no surprises

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral from my GP or paediatrician?+
No referral is needed. Parents and carers can contact us directly, and we also accept referrals from schools, GPs, paediatricians and other professionals.
Where do sessions take place?+
We offer flexibility — sessions can take place at our clinic, in your child's school, or in your home, depending on what best suits their goals.
Can you complete an assessment for an EHCP application?+
Yes. We regularly produce independent occupational therapy reports for Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) applications and tribunals, written to meet local authority requirements.
What age range do you work with?+
We work with children and young people from age 2 through to 18, adapting our approach to suit each developmental stage.
How long is the waiting list?+
Waiting times vary by service and location. Our free phone consultation is the quickest way to get an accurate estimate for your family.
