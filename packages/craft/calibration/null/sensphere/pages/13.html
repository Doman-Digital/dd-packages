What is Occupational Therapy?

Turning everyday struggles into everyday skills

Occupational therapy for children focuses on the "occupations" that make up childhood — playing, learning, getting dressed, making friends, sitting still at the table, or coping with a noisy classroom. When these things feel harder than they should, it can affect confidence, behaviour and family life.

At Sensphere, our therapists look beyond the behaviour to understand the underlying sensory, motor, cognitive or emotional factors at play — and build a practical plan around your child's strengths, interests and goals.

Our approach is built on three things

  • Child-led, strengths-based practice — never a one-size-fits-all programme
  • Genuine partnership with parents, carers and school staff
  • Evidence-based tools, clearly explained in plain English
Who We Help

Support for a wide range of needs

We work with children and young people aged 2–18, whether or not they have a diagnosis, including those who are:

Sensory Processing Differences

Over- or under-sensitivity to sound, touch, movement or light that affects daily routines, sleep or eating.

Autism & ADHD

Individualised support for regulation, attention, transitions and sensory needs, at a pace that suits your child.

Coordination Difficulties (DCD/Dyspraxia)

Building gross and fine motor skills for PE, playground games, dressing and daily self-care tasks.

Fine Motor Skills & Handwriting

Pencil grip, letter formation, scissor skills and the strength and control needed for written work.

Emotional Regulation & Anxiety

Practical, sensory-informed strategies for meltdowns, shutdowns, anxiety and big feelings.

School Readiness & Transitions

Preparing for nursery, starting school, or moving between key stages with confidence and the right support in place.

Our Services

Assessment and therapy tailored to your child

Every service can be delivered at our clinic, in your home, in school, or online — whatever works best for your family.

Initial OT Assessment

A comprehensive assessment combining standardised tools, clinical observation and information from you and your child's school, resulting in a clear written report.

1:1 Therapy Sessions

Regular, goal-focused sessions designed around your child's specific needs — building skills through play and meaningful activity.

School Consultation & Staff Training

Classroom observations, environment reviews and practical staff training so support continues consistently during the school day.

EHCP & Tribunal Reports

Independent occupational therapy reports to support EHC needs assessments, annual reviews and, where needed, SEND tribunals.

Sensory Circuits & Group Programmes

Small, friendly group sessions that build regulation, coordination and social skills alongside other children.

Parent & Carer Coaching

Practical strategies and coaching so the progress made in sessions carries through into everyday family life.

Meet the Team

Experienced, HCPC-registered occupational therapists

Sensphere was founded by a team of paediatric occupational therapists with backgrounds in NHS community services, mainstream and specialist schools. We believe every child deserves a therapist who takes the time to understand them as an individual, not just a diagnosis on a form.

All Sensphere therapists are registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC), hold enhanced DBS checks, and engage in regular continuing professional development in sensory integration, DCD, and neurodevelopmental approaches.
