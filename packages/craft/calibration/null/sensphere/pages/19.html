About Sensphere

Practical support, rooted in understanding each child

Sensphere was founded by a team of paediatric occupational therapists who wanted to make specialist support more accessible to families across the UK. We work with children and young people aged 2 to 18 who are experiencing difficulties with sensory processing, motor coordination, self-regulation, handwriting, or daily living skills such as dressing and feeding.

Every plan starts with listening — to parents, to teachers, and to the child themselves. We believe therapy works best when it's woven into everyday routines at home and at school, not confined to a clinic room.

Our therapists hold postgraduate training in sensory integration and hold current HCPC registration. We accept referrals directly from families, as well as from GPs, paediatricians, schools and SENCOs.

What We Offer

Assessment and therapy, tailored to each child

From first assessment through to ongoing therapy and school support, we build a plan around what matters most for your child's day-to-day life.

OT Assessment

A comprehensive assessment of sensory processing, motor skills, and daily function, using standardised tools and clinical observation.

1:1 Therapy Sessions

Regular, goal-led sessions in clinic, at home, or online — building skills through play and structured activity.

Sensory Integration

Structured, play-based intervention for children who are over- or under-responsive to movement, touch, sound or texture.

Handwriting & Fine Motor

Targeted programmes to build pencil grip, letter formation, scissor skills and classroom fine motor confidence.

Family & Parent Coaching

Practical strategies for meal times, dressing, sleep and transitions, so support continues between sessions.

School Consultations

On-site visits, EHCP-related reports, and staff training to help classroom teams put strategies into practice.

How It Works

A clear path from first contact to progress

We keep the process simple and transparent, so families know what to expect at every stage.

1

Enquiry & referral

Get in touch directly, or ask your GP, paediatrician or school to refer your child to us.

2

Assessment

Your therapist meets your child, gathers background, and completes a full occupational therapy assessment.

3

Personalised plan

You receive a written report with clear goals and strategies for home, and for school if relevant.

4

Ongoing therapy

Regular sessions track progress, with reviews to adjust the plan as your child develops.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral from a GP or paediatrician?

No — families can contact us directly without a referral. We also accept referrals from GPs, paediatricians, health visitors and schools, and are happy to liaise with any professionals already involved.

What age range do you work with?

We support children and young people from age 2 to 18, adapting our assessment tools and therapy approach to suit each stage of development.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer appointments at our clinic, in your home, at school, or online via video call, depending on what best suits your child's needs and goals.

How long does the process usually take?

Most families begin with an assessment appointment, followed by a written report within two weeks. Ongoing therapy is reviewed regularly, and the length of support varies depending on individual goals.

Can you provide reports for EHCP applications?

Yes. We regularly write occupational therapy reports to support Education, Health and Care Plan applications, tribunals, and annual reviews.
