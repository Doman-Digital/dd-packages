What we offer

Assessments and therapy that fit real life

We offer a full pathway from initial assessment through to ongoing therapy, delivered in clinic, at home, or in school.

Occupational Therapy Assessment

A comprehensive assessment of your child's motor skills, sensory processing, and daily functioning, using standardised tools and clinical observation.

  • Fine and gross motor skills
  • Sensory processing profile
  • Self-care and independence skills
  • Detailed written report with recommendations

Sensory Integration Therapy

One-to-one therapy for children who struggle to process sensory information, using structured, play-based sensory activities.

  • Regulation and self-calming strategies
  • Sensory diet planning
  • Progress reviewed every term

Handwriting & Fine Motor Groups

Small-group and individual sessions building the pencil control, hand strength, and posture that underpin confident writing.

  • Letter formation and pencil grip
  • Bilateral coordination
  • School-aligned targets

School & EHCP Consultation

We work directly with schools to translate assessment findings into workable classroom strategies and EHCP-ready reports.

  • Classroom observation visits
  • Staff training sessions
  • Reports for EHCP and tribunal purposes

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral to book an assessment?

No referral is needed. Parents and carers can contact us directly, and schools can also refer a pupil with parental consent.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer assessments and therapy in our clinic, in your child's school, or at home, depending on what suits your family and what the assessment requires.

Can you provide reports for an EHCP application?

Yes. Our assessment reports are written to a standard suitable for EHCP applications, annual reviews, and, where needed, tribunal proceedings.

How long does an assessment take?

A typical initial assessment takes 60–90 minutes, with the written report following within two to three weeks.

Do you offer sessions outside school hours?

Yes, we offer some after-school and school holiday appointments alongside our daytime clinic and in-school sessions.
