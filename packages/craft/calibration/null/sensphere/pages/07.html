What we offer

Assessment and therapy built around each child

Every child develops differently. We combine standardised assessment with everyday, play-based therapy so progress happens at home, in the classroom and beyond.

Full OT Assessments

Comprehensive assessments of sensory processing, fine and gross motor skills, self-care and daily living skills, using standardised tools and clinical observation.

Sensory Processing Support

Identifying sensory sensitivities and seeking behaviours, and building practical sensory diets and strategies for home, school and the community.

Fine Motor & Handwriting

Therapy programmes to build pencil grip, hand strength, coordination and handwriting fluency, tracked against clear, achievable goals.

Self-Regulation & Emotional Wellbeing

Helping children recognise and manage their emotions and energy levels, building resilience for the classroom, home and friendships.

School-Based Support

Classroom observations, staff training, sensory circuits and workstation set-up, plus written reports for EHC plans, SENCos and tribunals.

Parent & Family Coaching

Guided coaching sessions so parents and carers feel confident using strategies day-to-day, without needing to be in every therapy session.

How it works

A clear pathway from first contact to progress

We keep the process simple and jargon-free, so you always know what happens next.

01

Free Initial Chat

A 15-minute call to talk through your concerns and decide together whether an assessment is the right next step.

02

Assessment

A detailed assessment session with your child, plus questionnaires for parents and, where useful, teachers.

03

Report & Plan

A written report with clear findings and a personalised therapy or support plan, explained in a follow-up call.

04

Ongoing Therapy

Regular sessions at our clinic, at home, in school or online, with progress reviewed every six to eight weeks.

About Sensphere

Founded by a paediatric occupational therapist, built around families

Sensphere was founded to close the gap between long NHS waiting lists and the everyday support families need. Our team of HCPC-registered occupational therapists specialises exclusively in children and young people, drawing on experience across NHS community paediatrics, special educational needs schools and private practice.

We believe the best outcomes come from working alongside the people who know a child best — parents, carers and teachers — not from therapy that happens in isolation.

  • Registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC)
  • Members of the Royal College of Occupational Therapists (RCOT)
  • Enhanced DBS checked and fully insured
  • Experience with autism, ADHD, DCD/dyspraxia and sensory processing differences

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a GP or paediatrician referral?

No. Parents and carers can self-refer directly, and we also accept referrals from schools, GPs, paediatricians and local authorities.

How long does an assessment take?

A full OT assessment typically takes 90 minutes with your child, followed by parent and, where relevant, teacher questionnaires. Your written report usually follows within two to three weeks.

Can reports be used for an EHC needs assessment?

Yes. Our reports are written to a standard suitable for supporting Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) applications, reviews and tribunals, and we're happy to liaise directly with your child's school or local authority with your consent.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer sessions at our clinic base, in your child's school, at home, or via secure video call, depending on what suits your child and family best.

What areas do you cover?

In-person visits cover Surrey, Hampshire, and the wider South East of England. Assessments, therapy and parent coaching are also available UK-wide via telehealth.

How much does it cost?

Costs depend on the type and length of session. We'll always confirm pricing clearly before booking, and can advise on funding routes such as Personal Health Budgets or Direct Payments where applicable.

Ready to talk about your child?

Book a free 15-minute call with one of our therapists. No obligation, no jargon — just a chance to talk through what's going on and what might help.
