About Sensphere

Child-led therapy, grounded in real life.

Sensphere was founded by a team of paediatric occupational therapists who believe therapy should fit around a child's real life — their classroom, their bedroom, their playground — not the other way round. We look beyond the behaviour to understand the sensory, motor and emotional regulation skills underneath it.

We work with children and young people aged 2 to 18, alongside parents, carers, teachers and SENCOs, to build practical strategies that stick — at home, at school and in the community.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a referral to book an assessment?

No. Most families come to us directly without a GP or paediatrician referral, though we're happy to work alongside any professionals already involved with your child.

Where do sessions take place?

We offer assessments and therapy in our clinic rooms, in schools by arrangement, at home for younger children where appropriate, and via secure video call for follow-up and coaching sessions.

How long does an assessment take?

Our comprehensive OT assessment typically takes 90 minutes with your child, followed by a written report within two to three weeks.

Can your reports be used for an EHCP or tribunal?

Yes. Our reports are written to a standard suitable for Education, Health and Care Plan needs assessments, annual reviews and SEND tribunal evidence.

What areas do you cover?

We see families across England and Wales through our clinic and school visits, plus UK-wide via video consultation for coaching and review sessions.

How much does therapy cost?

Fees depend on the type and length of service. We'll always confirm pricing clearly before booking, with no hidden costs.

Book a free 15-minute call and we'll help you find the right service for your child.
