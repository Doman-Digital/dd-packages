What We Offer

Our services

From initial assessment through to ongoing therapy, we tailor every step to the child in front of us — not a generic programme.

OT Assessments

Comprehensive, standardised and observational assessments covering sensory processing, motor skills, self-care and school-based function, with a clear written report.

1:1 Therapy Sessions

Individualised, play-based therapy programmes at home, in clinic or at school, targeting the specific goals identified for each child.

Reports for EHCPs & Tribunals

Detailed, evidence-based reports to support Education, Health and Care Plan applications, annual reviews and SEND tribunal cases.

School Consultations

On-site visits, staff training and practical strategies for SENCOs, teachers and teaching assistants to support pupils in the classroom.

Parent & Carer Coaching

Guidance and coaching sessions so families feel confident using strategies at home, and understand the reasons behind their child's behaviour.

Sensory Circuits & Groups

Small-group movement and regulation sessions designed to help children arrive at learning ready to focus and engage.

Getting Started

How the process works

Every family's route into Sensphere looks a little different, but the process generally follows four steps.

1

Get in touch

Contact us directly or ask your child's school, GP or SENCO to make a referral on your behalf.

2

Assessment

We meet your child at home, school or clinic, gather background information, and complete relevant assessments.

3

Report & plan

You receive a clear written report with findings and a therapy plan built around realistic, meaningful goals.

4

Therapy & review

We deliver ongoing sessions, check progress regularly, and adjust the plan as your child develops.

  • Child-centred, not one-size-fits-all
  • Play-based and strengths-focused
  • Grounded in current research
  • Built around real family routines
  • Close collaboration with schools
About Sensphere

Occupational therapy that fits around your child's world

We started Sensphere because too many families were being handed generic advice sheets instead of a plan built around their own child. Our therapists spend time understanding what a good day and a hard day actually look like — at home, in the playground, at the dinner table, in the classroom — before recommending anything.

Every assessment and therapy session is led by a qualified, HCPC-registered occupational therapist with specific experience in paediatrics. We keep caseloads manageable so we can give each family the attention they need, and we stay in regular contact with teachers and SENCOs so strategies are consistent wherever your child spends their day.
