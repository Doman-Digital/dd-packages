What We Do

Electrical services for every job, big or small

From a single faulty socket to a full rewire or shop fit-out, our qualified electricians handle domestic and commercial work to the current wiring regulations (BS 7671).

Full & Partial Rewires

Complete or partial house rewires carried out with minimal disruption, dust sheets down and mess cleared up before we leave.

Consumer Unit Upgrades

Replace an old fuse box with a modern, RCD-protected consumer unit to bring your home or premises up to current safety standards.

EV Charger Installation

OZEV-approved home and workplace charge point installation, from initial survey through to commissioning.

Fault Finding & Repairs

Tripping breakers, flickering lights or dead sockets diagnosed quickly and fixed properly — not just patched over.

EICR & Landlord Certificates

Electrical Installation Condition Reports for landlords, homeowners and businesses, with same-day certificates where possible.

Lighting Installation

Downlights, pendant fittings, outdoor and security lighting, and garden lighting, designed and fitted to suit your space.

Smoke & Heat Alarms

Mains-wired, interlinked smoke, heat and carbon monoxide alarms fitted to current building regulations.

Commercial Maintenance

Reactive and planned electrical maintenance contracts for offices, shops, salons and small industrial units.

PAT Testing

Portable appliance testing for landlords and businesses, with detailed reports and re-test reminders.
