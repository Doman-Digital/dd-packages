Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Our Service

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Uxbridge and cover the surrounding areas of West London, plus Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire. If you're unsure whether we cover your postcode, just give us a call.

Are your electricians qualified and insured?

Yes. All our electricians are NICEIC-registered, Part P certified, and fully insured, so every job is compliant with UK wiring regulations and covered by public liability insurance.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No. We don't charge a call-out fee for standard quotes. For genuine emergencies outside normal hours, a small out-of-hours rate applies, which we'll always confirm with you before attending.

How quickly can you come out?

For non-urgent work we typically book within a few days. For emergencies, such as power loss or exposed wiring, we aim to attend the same day, 24 hours a day.

Do you provide certificates for completed work?

Yes, every job comes with the relevant certification, such as a Minor Works Certificate, Electrical Installation Certificate, or EICR, depending on the work carried out.

Can you help with commercial or business premises?

Absolutely. We regularly work with small businesses, shops, salons and offices on jobs including PAT testing, emergency lighting, and full or partial rewires.
