What We Do

Electrical services for every job, big or small

From a single socket to a full rewire, our qualified electricians handle domestic and commercial electrical work across the region.

Domestic Electrics

Rewires, consumer unit upgrades, socket and lighting installations, and general repairs for homes of every size.

Commercial & Small Business

Office fit-outs, shop and salon rewiring, PAT testing and EICR reports to keep your business safe and compliant.

Emergency Call-Outs

Power cuts, tripped circuits, or a burning smell from a socket — our emergency team responds around the clock.

EICR & Fault Finding

Electrical Installation Condition Reports for landlords and homeowners, plus fast, accurate fault diagnosis.

Lighting Design & Install

Downlights, garden lighting, smart switches and dimmers, installed neatly and wired to last.

EV Charger Installation

OZEV-approved home and workplace EV charge point installation, with all paperwork and grants handled for you.
