What We Do

Electrical services for every job, big or small

Whether it's a flickering light in the kitchen or a full electrical fit-out for a new shop unit, our qualified electricians handle it with the same care and attention to detail.

Domestic Electrics

Rewires, consumer unit upgrades, sockets, switches, lighting and general electrical repairs for houses and flats.

Commercial & Small Business

Office and shop fit-outs, emergency lighting, fire alarm interfacing and periodic testing for local businesses.
