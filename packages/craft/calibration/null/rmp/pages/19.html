What We Do

Electrical services for homes and small businesses

Whatever the size of the job, our electricians are qualified to carry out work to the current BS 7671 wiring regulations, with certification provided for every job.

Full & Partial Rewires

Complete or partial rewires for period properties, renovations and extensions, with minimal disruption and full certification.

Consumer Unit Upgrades

Replacement of old fuse boxes with modern, RCD-protected consumer units to bring your home up to current safety standards.

EV Charger Installation

OZEV-approved home and workplace EV charge point installation, wired safely and ready for grant applications.

EICR & Safety Inspections

Electrical Installation Condition Reports for homeowners, landlords and businesses, with clear reporting and remedial work.

Fault Finding & Repairs

Tripping circuits, dead sockets, flickering lights — we diagnose the cause quickly and fix it properly, not just patch it over.

Lighting Installation

Downlights, pendant fittings, outdoor and security lighting, plus dimmer and smart lighting control upgrades.

Sockets, Switches & Circuits

Additional sockets, USB outlets and new circuits for kitchens, home offices, garages and outbuildings.

Small Business Electrics

Shop, office and unit fit-outs, emergency lighting, fire alarm testing and PAT testing for local businesses.

Landlord Certificates

EICRs and PAT testing for rental properties, with fast turnaround to keep you compliant and lettings moving.
