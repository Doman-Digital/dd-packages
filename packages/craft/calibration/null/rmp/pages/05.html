Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are you fully qualified and insured?

Yes. RMP Electrical is a NICEIC Approved Contractor and Part P registered, meaning our work is regularly audited and self-certified to building regulations. We hold £2 million public liability insurance.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Uxbridge and regularly work across West and Central London, Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire. If you're unsure whether we cover your postcode, just ask when you call or use the quote form.

Do you offer emergency callouts?

Yes, we offer a 24/7 emergency callout service for urgent issues such as power loss, tripping circuits or exposed wiring. Call us directly and we'll aim to get an engineer to you as soon as possible.

How much will my job cost?

Every job is different, so we provide a written, fixed-price quote before any work starts — no hourly rates and no surprises on the final invoice. Quotes are free and come with no obligation.

Will I get a certificate for the work?

Yes. All notifiable work is certified and, where applicable, registered with the relevant building control body. You'll receive copies for your records, mortgage provider, or letting agent.
