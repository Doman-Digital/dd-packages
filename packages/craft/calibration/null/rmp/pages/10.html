FAQs

Common questions

Are you fully qualified and insured?

Yes. RMP Electrical is a NICEIC approved contractor, and all our engineers are fully qualified and hold current Part P certification. We also carry full public liability insurance.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Uxbridge and cover the whole of London, plus Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire. If you're just outside these areas, give us a call — we may still be able to help.

Do you offer emergency callouts?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency callouts for urgent issues such as power loss, tripping circuits or safety hazards. Call our emergency line and we'll aim to be with you as quickly as possible.

How much will my job cost?

Every job is different, so we provide a free, fixed-price quote after understanding what you need. There are no hidden charges — the price we quote is the price you pay.

Do you work with small businesses?

Absolutely. We regularly carry out electrical maintenance, testing and installation work for shops, offices, salons and other small businesses, scheduling work to minimise disruption.
