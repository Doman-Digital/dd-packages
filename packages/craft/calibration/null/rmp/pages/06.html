FAQs

Common questions

Are your electricians qualified and insured?

Yes. All our electricians hold NVQ Level 3 qualifications, are Part P registered, and work to BS 7671 wiring regulations. RMP Electrical carries £5 million public liability insurance on every job.

How quickly can you attend an emergency call-out?

For urgent electrical faults within Uxbridge and the immediate surrounding area, we aim to attend the same day. Further afield in Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire, response time is typically within 24 hours.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes, all quotes are free and provided in writing before any work begins, so there are no surprises on your invoice.

Do you work with small businesses as well as homeowners?

Absolutely. Alongside domestic work, we regularly carry out electrical installations, testing and maintenance for offices, shops, salons and hospitality businesses.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Uxbridge and cover London, Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire, including Slough, Reading, Windsor, High Wycombe, Beaconsfield and Oxford.

Will I receive a certificate for the work carried out?

Yes, every job is completed with the appropriate electrical certification, such as a Minor Works Certificate, Electrical Installation Certificate, or EICR, emailed to you on completion.
