FAQs

Common questions

Are you insured and qualified?

Yes — RMP Electrical is an NICEIC Approved Contractor, Part P registered, and carries £2 million public liability insurance. Every electrician on our team holds a full 18th Edition qualification.

Do you offer emergency callouts?

Yes, we offer a 24/7 emergency callout service across all four counties we cover, for issues like power loss, sparking sockets or exposed wiring.

How much does an EICR cost?

Pricing depends on property size and number of circuits. We'll confirm an exact fixed price before booking — get in touch with your postcode and property type for a quote.

Do you work on small commercial units?

Absolutely — we regularly carry out fit-outs, PAT testing and emergency lighting for shops, offices and small industrial units across our coverage area.

How quickly can you come out?

For non-emergency work we typically quote within 24 hours and can usually book jobs in within a week. Emergencies are prioritised and often attended same-day.
