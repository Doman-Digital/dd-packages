What We Do

Electrical services for homes and small businesses

Whether it's a quick repair or a full rewire, our team handles domestic and light commercial electrical work to the same high standard.

Domestic Rewiring

Full and partial house rewires for period properties, extensions and renovations, finished with minimal disruption and mess.

EICR & Safety Inspections

Electrical Installation Condition Reports for homeowners, landlords and buy-to-let properties, with clear reporting and remedial work if needed.

EV Charger Installation

OZEV-approved home EV charge point installation, wiring and consumer unit upgrades to keep your car charging safely overnight.

Fault Finding & Repairs

Tripping circuits, flickering lights or dead sockets diagnosed and fixed fast, usually on the same visit.

Lighting & Sockets

Downlights, outdoor lighting, extra sockets and USB points, fitted neatly with matching finishes throughout your home.

Small Business Electrics

Office and retail fit-outs, emergency lighting, PAT testing and periodic inspections to keep your business compliant.
