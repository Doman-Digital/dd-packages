Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are you qualified and insured?

Yes. All our electricians are Part P registered and every job is covered by £2 million public liability insurance. We issue the correct certification for all notifiable work.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Uxbridge and cover the surrounding areas of London, Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire. If you're just outside these areas, contact us — we can often still help.

Do you work with small businesses as well as homeowners?

Yes, alongside domestic work we regularly carry out electrics for shops, salons, offices and other small commercial premises, including emergency lighting and fixed wire testing.

How quickly can you get to an emergency call-out?

For genuine electrical emergencies we aim to be with you within a few hours, any day of the week, via our 24/7 call-out line.

Do you provide written quotes before starting work?

Always. We'll assess the job and send a clear written quote before any work begins, so there are no surprises on the invoice.
