What We Do

Electrical services for homes and small businesses

Every job, big or small, is carried out to BS 7671 wiring regulations and signed off with the correct certification.

Domestic Rewiring

Full and partial rewires for period properties, extensions and renovations, with minimal disruption and dust-controlled working.

Consumer Unit Upgrades

Replace outdated fuse boxes with modern, RCD-protected consumer units to keep your property safe and insurance-compliant.

EICR & Landlord Certificates

Electrical Installation Condition Reports for landlords, homeowners and letting agents, usually turned around within 48 hours.

EV Charger Installation

OZEV-approved home and workplace EV charge point installation, from initial survey through to final commissioning.

Commercial & Retail Fit-Outs

Full electrical fit-outs for shops, offices, salons and hospitality units, including lighting, power and data first-fix.

Lighting & Smart Home

Downlights, garden lighting, dimmer circuits and smart home integration for a home that works the way you want it to.

Fault Finding & Repairs

Tripping breakers, dead sockets or flickering lights — our diagnostic tools track down the cause quickly and fix it properly.

Fire & Emergency Lighting

Design, installation and testing of fire alarm and emergency lighting systems for small business premises.

24/7 Emergency Call-Out

Power outage or safety concern? Our emergency line is answered around the clock across our full coverage area.
