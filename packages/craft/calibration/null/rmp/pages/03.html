What We Do

Electrical services for every job, big or small

Fully qualified electricians handling domestic and commercial work across the region, from a single socket to a full rewire.

Domestic Electrical Work

Socket and lighting installs, extra circuits, fault finding and general household electrics carried out to current wiring regulations.

Rewires & Upgrades

Partial and full house rewires, consumer unit and fuse board upgrades, and old wiring replacement for period and modern properties.

EV Charger Installation

OZEV-approved home and workplace EV charge point installation, wall-mounted or on driveways, with smart charger options.

EICR & Certification

Electrical Installation Condition Reports for landlords, homeowners and businesses, plus PAT testing and compliance certificates.

Commercial Electrical

Office, retail and small business electrics — lighting upgrades, emergency lighting, distribution boards and periodic testing.

Emergency Call-Outs

Power outages, tripping circuits or electrical faults — our on-call electricians respond fast, day or night.
