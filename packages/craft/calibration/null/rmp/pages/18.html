What We Do

Electrical Services for Every Job, Big or Small

Whether you're a homeowner, landlord, or small business owner, our qualified electricians handle installation, repair, and safety work to the highest standard.

Consumer Unit Upgrades

Replace an outdated fuse box with a modern, RCD-protected consumer unit for better protection against electric shock and fire.

Full & Partial Rewires

Complete rewiring for older properties or targeted rewiring for extensions, renovations and loft conversions.

EV Charger Installation

OZEV-approved home and workplace charge point installation, including consumer unit assessment and load calculations.

Lighting Installation

Indoor and outdoor lighting design and fitting, from downlights and LED upgrades to garden and security lighting.

EICR Safety Inspections

Electrical Installation Condition Reports for homeowners, landlords and businesses to satisfy legal and insurance requirements.

Fault Finding & Repairs

Tripping circuits, power outages, flickering lights — we diagnose and fix electrical faults quickly and correctly first time.

Landlord Safety Certificates

EICRs, PAT testing and smoke/CO alarm compliance to keep rental properties safe and legally compliant.

Small Commercial Works

Office and shop fit-outs, emergency lighting, data cabling and periodic testing for small business premises.
