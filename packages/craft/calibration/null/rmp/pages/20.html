Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Are your electricians qualified and insured?

Yes. All our electricians work to NICEIC standards, hold Part P certification for domestic work, and we carry £2 million public liability insurance on every job.

Do you offer emergency call-outs?

Yes, we offer a 24-hour emergency call-out service for issues like total power loss, exposed wiring or tripping consumer units, across our full coverage area.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Uxbridge and cover West London, Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire. If you're just outside these areas, get in touch — we can often still help.

How much will my job cost?

Every job is different, so we provide a fixed, written quote after understanding the work involved — either over the phone or with a short site visit for larger jobs. There's no charge for a quote.

Do I get a certificate after the work?

Yes. All notifiable work is certified in line with Part P Building Regulations and registered with our scheme provider, with a copy sent to you by email.
