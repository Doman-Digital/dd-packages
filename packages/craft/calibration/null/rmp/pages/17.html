Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer emergency call-outs?

Yes. We offer same-day and emergency call-outs for urgent issues such as power loss, tripping circuits or exposed wiring, across Uxbridge and the surrounding areas.

Are you insured and certified?

Yes. RMP Electrical is NICEIC registered and fully insured, and every job is certified to current BS 7671 wiring regulations.

How far do you travel for jobs?

We're based in Uxbridge and regularly work across London, Berkshire, Buckinghamshire and Oxfordshire. If you're outside this area, get in touch and we'll let you know if we can help.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes, quotes are free and with no obligation. For larger jobs like rewires we'll usually visit in person to give you an accurate price.

Can you work with small businesses, not just homes?

Absolutely. We regularly carry out office and retail fit-outs, PAT testing, emergency lighting and commercial EICRs for local businesses.
