Questions

Good to know before you get in touch

Why is there a monthly fee instead of just paying once?

A website isn't a one-off product — it needs hosting, security updates and occasional changes to keep working properly and rank well on Google. The monthly plan covers all of that as standard, so nothing breaks quietly in the background and you're never quoted extra for small updates.

Can I cancel if it's not working out?

Yes. Every plan runs month to month with 30 days' notice to cancel — no lock-in contract. If you leave, we'll help you export your content and point your domain wherever you'd like next.

Do I own the website and the domain?

The domain is registered in your name from day one. The design and code are yours to take with you if you ever leave, though ongoing hosting and support are what the monthly plan provides while you're with us.

How long does it take to get a site live?

Typically two to three weeks from our first call, depending on how quickly we get your content and feedback back. Online shops usually take a little longer, around four weeks.

What if I already have a website?

We can take over hosting and support for an existing site in most cases, or rebuild it properly if it's outdated or slow. We'll tell you honestly which makes more sense on the call.
