Common questions

Good to know

Is there really no big upfront fee? +

Correct — there's no separate design bill to pay before we start. Your monthly plan covers the design and build of your site as well as hosting and ongoing support, so there's nothing extra to find upfront.

What happens if I want to cancel? +

All our plans run on a rolling 30-day basis. If you decide to cancel, just let us know — you'll keep ownership of your domain name and we'll help you export your content.

Do I own the website? +

Yes. The design, content and domain are yours. Your monthly plan pays for us to build it, host it and keep it maintained, rather than paying for the code itself outright.

How long does it take to launch? +

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within two to three weeks of our first call, depending on how quickly we get your content and feedback back from you.

What counts as a "small edit"? +

Things like updating opening hours, prices, adding a photo, editing text, or adding a new team member. Bigger jobs like new sections or extra functionality are quoted separately or covered under Premium.
