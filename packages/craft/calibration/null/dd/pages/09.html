Frequently asked questions

What happens if I want to cancel?

You can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice. We'll hand over your files, content and domain access — no lock-in, no hidden exit fees.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The design and content are yours. The monthly fee covers hosting, maintenance and support rather than a licence, so if you ever leave, you take everything with you.

How long does a new site take to build?

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within two weeks of our first call. Storefront sites with a full product catalogue typically take three to four weeks.

What if I already have a domain and hosting?

No problem — we can work with your existing domain, or move hosting over to us so everything is managed in one place. We'll talk you through the options.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes, you can move between Starter, Growth and Storefront at any time as your business grows. We'll only ever charge for the plan you're on.
