FAQ

Common questions

Why monthly, not a one-off payment?

A one-off build gets you a site on launch day. A monthly plan keeps it secure, backed up and updated for years afterwards — most sites that only get built once end up out of date within 12 months.

Can I cancel if I need to?

Yes. Our plans run month to month with no long-term contract — we ask for 30 days' notice so we can hand everything over cleanly.

Do I own the website?

Yes, the site and content are yours. If you ever move on, we'll export everything and help transfer your domain and hosting.

What if I need something outside my plan?

Small updates are included every month. For bigger jobs — a new section, a redesign, extra features — we'll always quote it separately before doing any work.

How long does a build take?

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within 2–4 weeks of our first call, depending on how quickly we get your content and feedback.
