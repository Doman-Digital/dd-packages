Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why a monthly plan instead of just paying for a website?

Most website problems happen after launch — software needs updating, content goes stale, hosting needs renewing. Our monthly plans cover all of that as standard, so your site keeps working without you having to think about it or find someone new every time something needs changing.

Can I cancel, and what happens to my site if I do?

Yes — plans run month to month after any minimum term, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. We'll export your content and help you move the domain and hosting elsewhere, no hard feelings.

How long does a new website take to build?

Most Starter and Growth sites are designed, built and live within 2 to 4 weeks from our first call, depending on how quickly we get content and feedback from you.

Do you write the content for us?

We can. We'll always help shape and edit your copy, and for an extra fee we can write it from scratch based on a short interview with you about your business.

What if I already have a domain name or an old website?

That's fine — we'll take over your existing domain and point it at the new site, and can migrate content across from an old site where it makes sense to keep it.

Do you only work with businesses in the UK?

We're based in Leeds and mainly work with UK small businesses, but we build and support clients remotely, so location isn't usually a barrier.
