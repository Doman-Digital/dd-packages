Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why is it a monthly plan rather than a one-off payment?

A website isn't a one-time job — it needs hosting, security patches, backups, and small updates over time. Bundling all of that into one monthly fee means you always know your site is looked after, without needing to call a developer every time something needs changing.

Can I cancel if I need to?

Yes. All plans run month to month with 30 days' notice to cancel. There's no long-term contract, and if you leave, we'll help you take your content with you.

How long does a website take to build?

Most Starter and Growth sites take 2–4 weeks from our first call to going live, depending on how quickly we receive your content and feedback. Online shops typically take 4–6 weeks.

Do you work with businesses outside your local area?

Yes — we work with small businesses right across the UK. All our calls, design reviews and support are handled remotely by phone, email and video call.

What if I already have a website?

We can often migrate your existing site onto one of our plans, rebuilding or refreshing it where needed, rather than starting completely from scratch. Mention this on your free call and we'll take a look.
