Common questions

\n
\n
\n
\n Do I own the website?\n

Yes. The site, its content and your domain name are yours. If you ever leave, we hand everything over in full — no lock-in.

\n
\n
\n What's included in the monthly plan?\n

Hosting, security updates, backups, uptime monitoring, technical support and a set number of content changes each month, depending on your plan.

\n
\n
\n How long does a build take?\n

Most sites go live within two to three weeks of starting, depending on how quickly we receive your content and feedback.

\n
\n
\n Can I cancel the monthly plan?\n

Yes, anytime, with 30 days' notice. There are no long-term contracts — we'd rather earn your business every month.

\n
\n
\n Do you help with photography or writing the words?\n

We can. Every plan includes guidance on what to write, and we offer optional photography and copywriting add-ons if you'd rather we handled it.

\n
\n
\n I already have a website — can you take it over?\n

Often, yes. We'll review your current site and either move it onto our hosting and plans, or rebuild it if that makes more sense.
