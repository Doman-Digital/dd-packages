Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why is there a monthly fee instead of just paying once?

A one-off build gets your site live, but websites need hosting, security patches, backups and small edits over time. The monthly plan covers all of that so your site keeps working properly, rather than slowly going out of date the way most "pay once" sites do.

Can I cancel if I want to move elsewhere?

Yes. There's no long lock-in on any plan — 30 days' notice and it's done. If you leave, we'll help you export your content and point your domain wherever you need.

What counts as an "update" each month?

Text changes, new photos, price or menu updates, adding a page, swapping an image — the small, regular tweaks most businesses need. Larger jobs like a full redesign are quoted separately.

Do I need to already have a domain name or branding?

No. If you don't have a domain, logo or existing content, we can help sort all three as part of the build — it just gets factored into your quote.

How long does a new site take?

Typically 2–4 weeks from our first call to launch, depending on how quickly we get content and feedback back and forth. Online shops usually take a little longer.
