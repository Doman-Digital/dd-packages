FAQ

Common questions

Why a monthly plan instead of a one-off price? +

A one-off build is only half the job — websites need hosting, security patches, backups and the odd content update to stay useful. Rolling that into one monthly fee means there's no separate hosting bill, no surprise "maintenance" invoice, and a small team on hand whenever you need something changed.

What happens if I want to leave? +

You can cancel with 30 days' notice on any plan. We'll hand over your domain and a full export of your site files and content so you're never locked in — although most clients stay simply because it's less hassle than switching.

What counts as an "update request"? +

Text changes, swapping photos, adding a new service or team member, updating prices or opening hours — the day-to-day stuff. Bigger jobs like adding a whole new section or an online shop are quoted separately, but we'll always tell you the cost before starting.

Do I own my website? +

Yes. Your domain, content and branding are yours. The site is hosted and maintained by us while you're on a plan, and we'll transfer everything to you or another provider if you ever decide to leave.

How long does a new build take? +

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within two to three weeks of our first call, assuming we get your logo, photos and any copy fairly promptly. Established plan builds typically take three to five weeks depending on scope.

Do you work with businesses outside Leeds? +

Yes — we're based in Leeds but work with small businesses right across the UK. Calls and reviews happen over video or phone, so location has never been a barrier.
