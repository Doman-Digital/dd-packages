Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Just get in touch — we're happy to talk it through.

Why monthly rather than a one-off build?

A website that isn't maintained slowly falls apart — plugins go out of date, security holes appear, and it drifts out of step with your business. A monthly plan means the build cost is spread out, and someone is always keeping it running, fast and secure.

Is there a contract, and can I leave?

Plans run on a 12-month minimum term to cover the design and build work, then continue monthly with 30 days' notice to cancel. There's no long-term lock-in beyond that first year, and you own your domain name throughout.

What happens if I stop paying?

If you ever want to leave, we'll give you an export of your content and point you toward hosting elsewhere. We'd rather earn your business every month than trap you in a contract.

Do you work with businesses outside my local area?

Yes — while we're based in the UK and love working with local businesses, most of our client calls happen over video and everything can be done remotely.

What if I need more updates than my plan includes?

Extra work beyond your monthly allowance is billed at a simple hourly rate, agreed with you before we start. Most clients find their plan's allowance covers what they need.

Can you move my existing website onto a plan?

Often, yes. We'll review your current site and either bring it under a maintenance plan as-is, or recommend a rebuild if it's built on something outdated or hard to maintain.
