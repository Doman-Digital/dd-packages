Frequently asked questions

What's included in the monthly fee?

Hosting on our UK servers, an SSL certificate, daily backups, security monitoring, and content updates (the amount depends on your plan). You'll never get a separate hosting bill or renewal notice from a third party.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The design, content, and domain belong to you. If you ever want to leave, we'll export your site files and hand over your domain, no questions asked.

What if I want to cancel?

There's no long-term contract. Give us 30 days' notice and we'll help you move your site elsewhere or wind things down cleanly.

How long does a new website take?

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within three to four weeks of our first call. Commerce sites with a shop or booking system usually take five to six weeks.

I already have a website — can you take it over?

Often, yes. We'll review your existing site and either bring it onto our hosting and support as-is, or suggest a rebuild if it's outdated or hard to maintain.

Do you work outside the UK?

We're a small studio and currently focus on businesses based in the UK, so we can meet in person where useful and keep support during UK working hours.
