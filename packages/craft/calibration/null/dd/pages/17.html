Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does the monthly fee actually cover? +

Hosting, an SSL certificate, backups, software and security updates, uptime monitoring, and a set number of content updates each month (the amount depends on your plan). It's the ongoing running cost of the website, not a rolling design fee.

Do I own my website? +

Yes. The site and its content are yours. If you ever want to leave, we'll package everything up and hand it over — we just ask for 30 days' notice so we can do it properly rather than in a rush.

Why is there a setup fee if it's a monthly plan? +

The setup fee covers the actual design and build work — writing the structure, designing the pages, and getting everything tested before launch. The monthly fee then covers hosting and keeping it maintained afterwards. It's cheaper upfront than a traditional agency quote, and there's nothing hidden later.

How long does it take to launch? +

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within 2–3 weeks of us receiving your content and photos. Established plans with a shop or booking system usually take 4–5 weeks.

I already have a website — can you take over hosting and updates? +

Often, yes. We'll review your current site first; if it's built on a platform we can support, we can move it across and put it on a monthly plan without a full rebuild. If it needs replacing, we'll tell you honestly.

What if I need more than my plan's content updates? +

Extra updates are billed at a simple hourly rate with no minimum charge, or you can move up a plan if you find you need more support regularly.
