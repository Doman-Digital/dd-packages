Common questions

Everything you might want to know before getting started.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The content, branding and domain are yours. The monthly plan covers the ongoing design work, hosting, security and support that keeps the site running and up to date.

What happens if I want to cancel?

After your initial 12-month term you can cancel anytime with 30 days' notice. We'll help you export your content and point your domain wherever you need it to go.

What counts as a "small update"?

Things like changing opening hours, updating prices, swapping photos, adding a page, or fixing a typo. Bigger requests, like a full redesign, are quoted separately.

How long does it take to launch?

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within 2–3 weeks of our first call, depending on how quickly we get content and feedback back and forth.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

We're based in the UK and focus on UK small businesses, so we understand local search, compliance basics like cookie notices, and how UK customers browse and book.

I already have a website. Can you take it over?

Often, yes. We'll review your current site and domain setup and let you know honestly whether it's worth migrating or better to rebuild from scratch.
