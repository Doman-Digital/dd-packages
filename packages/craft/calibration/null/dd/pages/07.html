Questions

Common questions

What happens if I want to cancel?

You can cancel any plan with 30 days' notice. We'll export your content and, where possible, help you move the site elsewhere — there's no lock-in and no cancellation fee.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The domain and site content are yours. Our monthly fee covers hosting, maintenance and support rather than ownership of the site itself.

How long does a build usually take?

Most Starter and Growth sites are ready to review within two weeks of our kick-off call, depending on how quickly we receive your content and feedback.

What if I need something outside my plan's included updates?

Small extras like an additional page or a bigger redesign are quoted separately and only carried out with your approval — we'll never bill you without agreeing it first.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

We're based in the UK and focus on UK small businesses, so we understand local search, compliance and customer expectations, but we're happy to discuss projects further afield.
