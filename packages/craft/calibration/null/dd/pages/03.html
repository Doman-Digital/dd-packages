Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain name and all your content belong to you. The monthly plan covers the ongoing hosting, updates, and support — if you ever leave, we hand everything over cleanly.

What happens if I want to cancel?

You can cancel your plan with 30 days' notice at any time. There are no long lock-in contracts — we just ask for enough notice to arrange a smooth handover of your files and domain.

How long does it take to build a site?

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within 2–3 weeks of our first call. Commerce sites with a full online shop typically take 4–6 weeks depending on the number of products.

What if I need changes after launch?

Every plan includes a set number of content edits each month — updating text, photos, prices, or opening hours. Bigger changes, like new pages or features, are quoted separately and fairly.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

We're a UK studio and focus on UK small businesses, which lets us understand local SEO, compliance, and customer expectations properly. We host everything on UK-based servers.
