Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why is there a setup fee if it's a monthly plan?

The setup fee covers the design, build and testing of your site — the same work you'd pay for with a traditional web design project. The monthly plan then covers hosting, updates, support and ongoing improvements, so you're never paying twice for the same thing.

What happens if I want to cancel?

You can cancel any time with 30 days' notice. We'll export your content and, if you'd like to move your domain elsewhere, help you do that too. There are no long-term contracts to break.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The site is built for your business and the domain is registered in your name. Your monthly plan simply keeps it hosted, secure and up to date while you're with us.

How many changes can I actually ask for?

Each plan includes a set number of content updates a month, such as text changes, new photos or price updates. If you need something bigger, like a new page or a redesign of a section, we'll quote it separately before doing any work.

Can you work with a business that already has a website?

Yes — a lot of our clients come to us with an outdated or slow site that hasn't been touched in years. We'll review what you have, migrate anything worth keeping, and rebuild the rest.

Do you work with businesses outside your local area?

Yes, we work with small businesses across the UK. Calls and meetings are usually done over video, with everything else handled by email and phone.
