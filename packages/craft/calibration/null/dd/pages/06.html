Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I own the website?

Yes. The design and content are yours. Hosting and the monthly support are provided by us as part of the plan, and we'll help you move the site elsewhere if you ever decide to leave.

What happens if I want to cancel?

All plans run on a rolling basis after the initial 12-month term, and you can cancel with 30 days' written notice. We'll export your content and help with the handover.

How long does it take to get a site built?

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within 2–3 weeks of us receiving your content and sign-off. Commerce sites with a shop usually take 3–4 weeks.

What if my edits go over the included hours?

We'll always let you know before doing extra work. Anything beyond your monthly allowance is billed at £35 per hour, agreed in advance — never a surprise on your invoice.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

We're based in the UK and focus on UK small businesses, but we're happy to have a conversation if you're based elsewhere.

Ready for a website that actually gets looked after?

Book a free 20-minute call. We'll ask about your business, look at what you have now (if anything), and tell you honestly what we'd recommend.
