Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why monthly instead of a one-off price?

Most agencies charge a big fee upfront and then leave you to sort hosting, security, and updates yourself. Our monthly plan covers the build, hosting, maintenance and small edits together — so your site keeps working, and keeps being looked after, long after launch.

Is there a contract?

No lock-in contracts. Plans run month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. We'd rather earn your business every month than tie you into something long.

What if I already have a website?

We can often move your existing content across to a new build, or take over hosting and maintenance of your current site if it's in good shape. Get in touch and we'll take a look for free.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and if you ever leave, we'll hand over your files and content so you can take them elsewhere.

How many changes are included each month?

It depends on your plan — Starter includes one update a month, while Growth and Established include unlimited small content updates (text, images, prices, opening hours). Bigger changes, like new page types, are quoted separately.

How long does it take to go live?

Most sites are designed, built and live within two to three weeks of our first call, depending on how quickly we get your content and any feedback back from you.
