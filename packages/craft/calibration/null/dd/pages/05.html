Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I own my website, or are we renting it from you?

The site is built for you and the design and content are yours. Your monthly plan covers hosting, maintenance and support — if you ever leave, we'll help you export your content and move on cleanly.

What happens if I want to cancel?

Just give us 30 days' notice. There are no lock-in contracts or cancellation fees on any of our plans.

How long does a new site take to build?

Most Starter and Growth sites go live within three weeks of our first call, depending on how quickly we get content and feedback back from you.

Can you work with a business that already has a website?

Yes — a lot of our clients come to us with an outdated or slow site. We'll review what you have, tell you honestly whether it's worth rebuilding, and take it from there.

What counts as a "content update"?

Things like changing text, swapping photos, updating prices or opening hours, or adding a new page. Larger changes like a full redesign are quoted separately.

Are you based in the UK?

Yes, Doman Digital is a small UK-based studio. We work with clients across the country by phone, video call and email.
