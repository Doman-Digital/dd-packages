Questions

Good to know

Why is there a monthly fee instead of just a one-off build cost?

A website isn't finished the day it goes live — software needs updating, certificates expire, and things need changing as your business grows. The monthly fee covers hosting, security, backups and a set number of updates, so there's never a surprise bill when something needs fixing.

Can I cancel if I want to?

Yes. There's no long-term contract — just 30 days' notice. If you cancel, we'll help you export your content and point you toward a new host.

Do I own my website?

Yes, the design and content are yours. We simply build, host and maintain it for you as part of your plan.

How do content updates work?

Email or message us what you'd like changed — new opening hours, a photo, a price — and we make the update for you. No logging into confusing software required.

I already have a website. Can you take it over?

Often, yes. We'll review your existing site and either bring it onto one of our plans as-is or suggest a rebuild if it needs more than light maintenance can fix.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

We're based in the UK and focus on UK small businesses, so we understand local search, local customers and UK hosting requirements.
