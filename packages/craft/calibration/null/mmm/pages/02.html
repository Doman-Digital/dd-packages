Our Studio

A calm space in the heart of Brackley

MMM Beauty was started with one simple idea: everyone deserves a bit of time to feel looked after, without having to travel far or book weeks in advance. We're a small, welcoming studio in Brackley, Northamptonshire, serving clients from the town and surrounding villages.

We keep things personal — you'll always be looked after by someone who knows your nails, your brows and your skin, appointment after appointment.

  • Quality, long-lasting gel and salon products
  • Relaxed, unrushed appointments
  • Easy online and phone booking
