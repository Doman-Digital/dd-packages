Our Story

A studio built on care, not just treatments

MMM Beauty opened its doors in Brackley with a simple idea: beauty appointments should feel like a treat, not a task. We're a small, dedicated team who genuinely love what we do, and it shows in the finish of every set of nails, every brow shape and every facial.

We use high-quality, kind-to-skin products and take the time to understand what you actually want — whether that's your go-to French manicure or a completely new look for a big occasion.

  • Fully qualified, insured therapists
  • Premium, cruelty-free products
  • Relaxed, unrushed appointments
  • Easy online & phone booking
