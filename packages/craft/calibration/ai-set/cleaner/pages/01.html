FAQs

Common questions

Do I need to supply cleaning products?
No — our cleaners bring professional-grade, eco-friendly products and equipment as standard. If you'd prefer we use your own supplies, just let us know when booking.
What areas around Reading do you cover?
We cover Reading town centre and surrounding areas including Caversham, Earley, Tilehurst, Woodley, Calcot, Wokingham and Shinfield. Not sure if we cover you? Just ask.
Is there a minimum contract length?
No. Regular domestic and office cleans run on a flexible, no-contract basis — pause, change frequency, or cancel with 48 hours' notice.
Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?
Yes. Every Brightside cleaner is DBS-checked and reference-verified, and all work is covered by our £5 million public liability insurance.
How quickly can you start?
For most domestic and office bookings we can arrange your first clean within 3-5 working days, subject to availability.
