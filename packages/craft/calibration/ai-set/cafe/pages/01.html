Our story

Started by two neighbours who wanted better coffee on the walk.

The Lantern opened in 2019 in a converted shopfront halfway up Leith Walk, when Edinburgh's answer to a decent flat white was still a bus ride away. We roast with a small supplier in Fife, bake most of our own pastries, and keep the menu short so everything on it is done properly.

We're dog-friendly, laptop-tolerant before noon, and busiest on Saturday mornings — come early or come for a late lunch if you'd rather have the place to yourself.
