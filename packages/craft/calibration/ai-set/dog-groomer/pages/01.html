About Scruffs to Fluffs

Where nervous first-timers become regulars who wag their tails at the door

Scruffs to Fluffs was founded by Danielle Whitfield in a small unit off Wellington Road South, with one simple idea: grooming should be calm, kind and completely on the dog's terms. Nearly a decade on, we're still a small, independent salon — no franchise, no rush, just a team that genuinely loves dogs.

We work with anxious rescues, boisterous puppies, senior dogs who need a softer touch, and everything in between. If your dog has had a bad grooming experience before, tell us — we'll build a plan around them, not the clock.

  • Fear-free, force-free handling techniques
  • One dog groomed at a time — no crating, no queues
  • Free local pickup and drop-off across Stockport
  • Natural, hypoallergenic products for sensitive skin
