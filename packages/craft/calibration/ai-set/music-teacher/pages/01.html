Frequently Asked Questions

What age can my child start lessons?

Most children start piano from around age 6 and singing from around age 7, once they have the concentration and physical coordination to get real benefit from lessons. I'm happy to have a short chat beforehand if you're not sure whether your child is ready.

Do I need my own piano or keyboard?

Yes, regular practice between lessons makes a big difference to progress. A full-size, weighted keyboard is a good minimum requirement for piano students; I'm happy to advise on suitable options if you're buying one.

Where do lessons take place?

In-person lessons are held at my teaching studio in central Cambridge, with free on-street parking nearby. Online lessons are available via Zoom for those who prefer to learn from home.

How do I pay for lessons?

Lessons are invoiced termly by bank transfer, in line with local school term dates. Full details are provided once your regular lesson slot is confirmed.

What if we need to cancel a lesson?

I ask for at least 24 hours' notice where possible. Lessons cancelled with less notice can't usually be refunded, but I'll always try to offer a makeup slot where I have availability.
