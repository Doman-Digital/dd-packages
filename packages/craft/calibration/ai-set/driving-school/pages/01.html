Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to decide between manual and automatic before I start?
Not necessarily. If you're unsure, mention it when you book and your instructor can talk through the pros and cons based on what you plan to drive after passing.
How many lessons will I need?
On average, DVSA data suggests around 45 hours of professional lessons plus 20 hours of private practice. It varies by pupil — your instructor will give you an honest estimate after your first couple of lessons.
Do you offer pickup from home or work?
Yes. Lessons include pickup and drop-off anywhere within our Coventry coverage area, at no extra cost.
Can I pay for lessons individually or do I have to buy a block?
Both options are available. Block bookings work out cheaper per hour, but single lessons are available if you'd prefer to try us out first.
Do you help book my theory and practical test?
Yes, we can advise on timing and, for intensive course pupils, we'll handle the practical test booking as part of the package.
