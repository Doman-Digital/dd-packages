About Castle Dental

A trusted, local practice for the whole of Warwick

Castle Dental was founded to give Warwick families a genuine choice: dependable NHS care alongside private treatments, all under one roof, delivered by a team who remember your name and your history.

We're based just a short walk from Warwick town centre, with easy parking for patients who prefer to drive. Our practice was designed to feel relaxed rather than clinical, because we know a trip to the dentist isn't everyone's favourite outing.

  • ✓ Experienced NHS and private dentists
  • ✓ Flexible appointments, including early mornings
  • ✓ Modern digital X-rays for lower radiation exposure
  • ✓ Nervous patient support and sedation options
Your Options

NHS or private — the choice is yours

We believe good dental care shouldn't be complicated. Here's a simple breakdown of what's available to you at Castle Dental.

NHS Dental Care

Band 1–3 / per NHS charge band
  • Check-ups, X-rays and preventative advice
  • Fillings, extractions and root canal treatment
  • Crowns, dentures and bridges
  • Charges set nationally by the NHS
  • Free for eligible children, pregnant women and exemption groups
