Our Studio

Ten years in Brighton, built on word of mouth.

Black Anchor opened in 2015 above a record shop on Sydney Street, and we've stayed close to that spirit ever since — a small, appointment-only studio where the work speaks louder than the marketing.

Every artist here specialises in a distinct style. We don't take walk-ins for anything beyond small flash pieces, because we'd rather spend proper time getting a design right than rush ten in a day.

What We Do

Styles We Specialise In

Each artist at Black Anchor has their own focus, so we can match you to whoever draws in the style you're after.

Fine Line

Delicate, single-needle linework for botanical, script, and minimalist designs.

Blackwork

Bold solid ink, heavy shading, and geometric patternwork with real staying power.

Traditional

Bold outlines and classic colour palettes — anchors, swallows, roses, and script.

Neo-Traditional

Traditional roots with richer colour, illustrative detail, and modern subject matter.

Japanese-Influenced

Large-scale composition and flow, built for sleeves, backs, and full bodysuits.

Cover-Ups & Reworks

Honest consultations on what's possible, and creative solutions for old tattoos.

How It Works

Getting a Tattoo at Black Anchor

Every custom piece follows the same four steps, from first message to healed ink.

01

Enquiry

Send us your idea, size, placement, and reference images through the contact form below.

02

Consultation

We book a short in-studio or video consult to talk design, placement, and pricing.

03

Design

Your artist drafts a custom design, with one round of revisions before your appointment.

04

Tattoo Day

We confirm final placement and sizing on the day, then get to work — plus full aftercare guidance.
