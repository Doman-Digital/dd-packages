About Motion Physio

Guildford-based, sports-trained, patient-focused

Motion Physio was founded in 2013 by a team of chartered physiotherapists who wanted Guildford to have a clinic that treated people, not just symptoms. Since then we've helped thousands of local runners, footballers, rugby players and office workers get back to doing what they love without pain.

Every treatment plan starts with a thorough assessment, a clear diagnosis explained in plain English, and a realistic timeline — so you always know what's happening and why.

  • ✓ HCPC-registered, Chartered Society of Physiotherapy members
  • ✓ On-site strength & conditioning gym for rehab
  • ✓ Direct billing with AXA, Bupa, Vitality & Aviva
  • ✓ Free 10-minute phone consultation before booking
