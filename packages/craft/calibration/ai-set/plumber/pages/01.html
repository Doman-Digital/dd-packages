About Tidewell

Local engineers, honest pricing, no jargon

Tidewell Plumbing was founded in Norwich by James Tidewell in 2009, and we're still based just off the Dereham Road. What started as a one-van operation is now a team of five Gas Safe registered engineers covering the whole city and out into the surrounding villages.

We're not a franchise chasing a sales target — we're a local business that depends on word of mouth, which is why every job gets a written quote, a tidy finish, and a follow-up call to make sure you're happy.
