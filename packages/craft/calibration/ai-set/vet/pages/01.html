A practice that still feels like Kendal

Meadowbank Vets was founded in 2009 by Dr Alison Reeve, a Kendal native who wanted a local alternative to the big corporate chains — somewhere pet owners could always see a familiar face and get a straight answer.

Seventeen years on, we're still independently owned, still based on Stramongate, and still built around the idea that good veterinary care starts with taking the time to listen.

  • ✓ Same vet for repeat visits wherever possible, so your pet's history is never lost
  • ✓ Transparent, itemised pricing with estimates given before treatment
  • ✓ Quiet, separate waiting areas for cats and dogs to reduce stress
  • ✓ Free nurse clinics for weight, nail and nutrition advice
