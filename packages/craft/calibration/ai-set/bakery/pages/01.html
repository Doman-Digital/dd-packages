Our Story

Started with one starter, one oven, and a lot of early mornings.

Crumb & Co began in 2018 in a tiny railway arch kitchen in Bedminster, when founder Alys Bevan started selling sourdough loaves from a trestle table at Tobacco Factory Market on Sunday mornings. The starter she fed by hand that first week — nicknamed Doreen — is still the one raising every loaf we bake today.

We moved into our current home on Chandos Road in 2021: a small shopfront with the ovens right behind the counter, so the smell of the morning's bake reaches the pavement before the door even opens. Everything is still made in small batches, by hand, using flour milled in the West Country wherever we can.
