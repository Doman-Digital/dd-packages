Our workshop

Run by mechanics, not a franchise

Spoke and Chain opened on Cowley Road in 2011 with two workstands and a lot of borrowed tools. Today it's a five-strong team of qualified mechanics who still do every repair by hand, on-site, in full view of the shop floor.

We stock parts for common repairs so most jobs are done same-day, and we're proud to be one of the few shops in Oxford still building wheels in-house.
