The Photographer

Hello, I'm Laura

I've spent the last nine years photographing weddings in the fields, farmhouses and honey-stone villages of the Cotswolds — and I still get the same flutter of excitement every single time.

My approach is unobtrusive. I'm not one for lengthy posed line-ups; instead, I spend the day watching for the moments that actually matter — your dad's hands shaking as he does up your buttons, the speech that goes slightly wrong, the first dance you didn't rehearse enough. Those are the photographs you'll still be looking at in thirty years.

I shoot on film and digital, work with a small and considered kit so I can move quietly through your day, and I always arrive with tea, not an agenda.

Laura x

How We Work Together

Coverage that fits your day

Every wedding is different, so every package starts as a conversation. Here's the shape most days take.

01

The Full Day

From the last curler in your hair through to your first dance — full coverage of getting ready, ceremony, speeches and the party, usually 10–12 hours.

02

The Ceremony & Party

For couples who'd rather I arrive as guests do. Coverage from just before the ceremony through to the first dance, roughly 6–8 hours.

03

Elopements

Small, intimate and often unlicensed ceremonies in beautiful corners of the Cotswolds. A few relaxed hours, just the two of you (or a handful of guests).
