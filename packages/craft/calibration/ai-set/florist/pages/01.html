Our Story

Rooted in York, grown from a love of seasonal flowers

Wild Stem started as a market stall on Newgate before finding a home just off Micklegate. We work with British growers wherever we can, favouring what's in season over what's flown in — which means your flowers change with the year, and they last.

We're a small team, which means the person who takes your call is often the same person tying your bouquet. It's how we keep every order personal, whether it's a single bunch or a full wedding.

— The Wild Stem team
