Our Studio

A quiet room off Cheltenham Parade

Still Point Yoga is a small, independent studio tucked just off the Stray, run by a single teacher who believes yoga works best in small groups, taught slowly, without ego or spectacle.

We keep our classes capped at eight students so there's time for proper hands-on adjustments, and so nobody feels lost at the back of the room. Most people who come here weren't "yoga people" before they started — that's rather the point.

"Come as you are. Leave a little steadier."

New to Yoga?

The Beginners' Course

Six weeks, one small group, no experience required. This course is designed to take you from your very first Downward Dog to feeling genuinely confident joining any class on our timetable.

  • Six consecutive weekly sessions of 75 minutes
  • Maximum of eight participants per course
  • Covers core postures, breathing, and basic relaxation
  • Free mat hire and a printed take-home practice sheet

Courses run each half-term on Monday evenings. Book early — they tend to fill up within a fortnight of being announced.

Your Teacher

Eleanor Marsh

Founder & Lead Instructor, 500hr RYT

Eleanor founded Still Point Yoga in 2012 after a decade of practising and teaching in London, drawn back to Yorkshire by the quiet of the Dales and the promise of a slower pace of life.

She trained originally in Hatha and Vinyasa traditions, later adding a Yin Yoga certification and a diploma in yoga for older adults. Her classes are known for being unhurried, precise, and refreshingly free of jargon.

When she's not teaching, you'll usually find her walking the Nidderdale AONB with an overly enthusiastic spaniel.

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be flexible to start?

Not at all — that's one of the most common myths about yoga. Flexibility is a result of practice, not a prerequisite for it. Most people who join our beginners' course have never touched their toes.

What should I bring to my first class?

Just comfortable clothing you can move in and a bottle of water. We provide mats, blocks, straps, and blankets, so there's no need to buy anything before you start.

Is there parking near the studio?

Yes — there's free on-street parking on the surrounding roads after 6pm, and a short walk from the Jubilee car park during the day.

Can I try a class before committing to the beginners' course?

Absolutely. We offer a single taster session at £12 for any of our all-levels classes, so you can get a feel for the studio before signing up to a course.

What if I need to miss a week of the beginners' course?

Just let us know in advance where possible. We'll always try to find you a catch-up slot in another beginner-friendly class within the same term.

Ready to give it a try?

Drop us a message and we'll help you find the right class or the next beginners' course start date.
