About Greenline Landscapes

Local knowledge, honest advice, and gardens that last.

Greenline Landscapes was founded in Guildford with a simple aim: to give Surrey homeowners the same standard of design and craftsmanship you'd expect on a much bigger budget, delivered by people who actually turn up when they say they will.

Our small, permanent team of landscapers, groundworkers and planting specialists has worked on gardens of every size — from compact town courtyards in Woking to large country plots near Guildford and Dorking. We know Surrey's clay soils, its microclimates and its planning quirks, and we design with all of it in mind.

  • Free on-site consultation and no-obligation quote
  • Fixed-price contracts with no hidden extras
  • All work carried out by our own directly employed team
  • Member of the British Association of Landscape Industries
