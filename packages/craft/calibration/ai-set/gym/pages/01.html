Our Story

Built by lifters, for lifters

Iron Works opened in 2016 in a disused steel unit off Alma Street, and we've kept the bones of the building — exposed brick, girders, and a floor that's seen more chalk than paint. We're not a chain gym. There's no smoothie bar, no tanning beds, and no sales pitch waiting at the door.

What you'll find is proper equipment — power racks, deadlift platforms, and enough plates to go around — plus a small team of coaches who actually know your name and your numbers. Whether you're chasing your first pull-up or your next competition total, this is a gym built around getting stronger, safely and consistently.

What We Offer

Training that fits how you want to work

From solo barbell sessions to fully coached programmes, everything at Iron Works is built around strength — how you build it, keep it, and use it.

Open Gym Access

24/7 key-fob entry to our full floor of power racks, platforms, dumbbells up to 60kg, and a dedicated conditioning corner. Train on your own schedule, day or night.

Personal Training

One-to-one coaching with a qualified strength coach who builds your programme around your goals, your schedule, and your joints. Ideal for beginners and competitive lifters alike.

Small Group Coaching

Semi-private sessions of up to four people, run three times a week. Get hands-on coaching and the accountability of training alongside others, at a fraction of the cost of 1-to-1.

Powerlifting Programming

Remote or in-person programming for squat, bench, and deadlift, written and adjusted weekly by our coaches. Includes video form checks and meet-day support.

Beginner Foundations

A four-week introduction covering the big lifts, gym etiquette, and how to structure your own training. The starting point for every new member who's new to lifting.

Nutrition Check-ins

Fortnightly check-ins with a coach to keep your eating in line with your training goals — no fad diets, just consistent, sustainable habits.
