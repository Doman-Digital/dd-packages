Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you only work with businesses in Exeter?

Most of our clients are based in Exeter and the surrounding Devon area, but we work with businesses across the South West and beyond — everything can be handled by phone, email and video call where needed.

What software do you use?

We primarily work with Xero and QuickBooks, and can support most other major cloud accounting packages. If you're not using software yet, we'll help you get set up as part of onboarding.

How much does it cost to switch accountants?

Nothing. We handle the professional clearance and HMRC authorisation directly with your existing accountant, and there's no charge for the switch itself.

Can you help if I'm just starting out?

Yes — a good number of our clients come to us before they've even registered as self-employed or incorporated. We'll advise on the right structure from the outset.

Is the initial consultation really free?

Yes, with no obligation. It's a chance for us to understand your business and for you to decide if we're the right fit before any fees are agreed.
