About The Firm

A Wyle Cop firm, built on referrals rather than adverts.

Hale & Pryce was founded in 1987 by David Hale and Margaret Pryce, two Shrewsbury solicitors who believed clients deserved a fixed fee, a named point of contact, and a call back the same day. Nearly forty years on, those are still the rules the firm runs on.

We're based a short walk from the Square, opposite the old Music Hall, and most of our new clients still come to us because a neighbour, colleague or family member sent them our way.
