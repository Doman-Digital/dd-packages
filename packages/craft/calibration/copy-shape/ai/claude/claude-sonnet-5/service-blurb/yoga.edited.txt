Tucked away in the heart of Chester, Lotus Room welcomes yogis of every level, from total beginners to those who've been practising for years. Our studio is small by design, so you'll get real personal attention, a genuinely friendly community, and a calm space to reconnect with your breath and body. Fancy a gentle stretch and unwind, or want to build a stronger practice? Either way, our experienced instructors are right there with you.
