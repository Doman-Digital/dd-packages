Tucked away in the rolling countryside near Ludlow, Holloway Barn is a beautifully converted rustic venue that brings together timeless charm and modern comfort. Exposed beams, warm stone walls, and soft natural light set an intimate scene for both your ceremony and the celebration that follows. Whether you're planning a big, joyful bash or a smaller, more intimate gathering, our flexible indoor and outdoor spaces can flex to suit your day. And from the first look right through to the last dance, our experienced team will be right there with you, helping turn your vision into reality.
