Quiet Harbour Counselling offers one-to-one talking therapy for adults in Brighton, in a calm, confidential space where you can slow down and be heard. Whether you're working through anxiety, low mood, stress, or a difficult life transition, sessions are tailored to your pace and needs. Together we'll explore what's troubling you and build a clearer sense of direction, with warmth and without judgement. In-person and online appointments are available.
