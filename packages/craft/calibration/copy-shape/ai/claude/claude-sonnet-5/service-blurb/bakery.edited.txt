Rise is a small, independent bakery tucked away in the heart of Hebden Bridge, baking naturally leavened sourdough and handmade cakes fresh every day. Each loaf is slow-fermented for flavour, using simple, honest ingredients and nothing more. Alongside the bread, you'll find a rotating line-up of seasonal cakes and treats, whether you fancy something to eat in or take home. Pop in for a fresh loaf, a slice of cake, and a warm welcome.
