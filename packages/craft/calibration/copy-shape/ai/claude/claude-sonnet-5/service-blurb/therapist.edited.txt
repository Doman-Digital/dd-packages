At Quiet Harbour Counselling, I offer one-to-one talking therapy for adults in Brighton, in a calm, confidential space where you can slow down and actually be heard. Whether you're dealing with anxiety, low mood, stress, or going through a difficult life transition, sessions are shaped around your pace and what you need. Together, we'll explore what's troubling you and work towards a clearer sense of direction, with warmth and no judgement. In-person and online appointments are both available.
