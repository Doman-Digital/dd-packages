Set amidst the rolling countryside near Ludlow, Holloway Barn offers a beautifully converted rustic venue for weddings that blend timeless charm with modern comfort. Exposed beams, warm stone walls, and soft natural light create an intimate backdrop for your ceremony and celebration. Our flexible spaces accommodate both grand celebrations and more intimate gatherings, with dedicated indoor and outdoor areas for every part of your day. From your first look to the last dance, our experienced team is on hand to help bring your vision to life.
