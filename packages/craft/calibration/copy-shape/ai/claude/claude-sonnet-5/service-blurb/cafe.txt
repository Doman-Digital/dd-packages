Nestled in the heart of Whitby, The Copper Kettle is a cosy neighbourhood cafe serving freshly brewed coffee, homemade cakes, and hearty light bites. We source local ingredients wherever possible, from Yorkshire roasters to nearby bakers, so every cup and plate feels like home. Whether you're stopping in after a walk along the harbour or settling in with a book, our warm, relaxed space welcomes you to slow down and stay a while.
