Tucked away in the heart of Whitby, The Copper Kettle is a cosy neighbourhood cafe dishing up freshly brewed coffee, homemade cakes, and hearty light bites. We try to source local wherever we can, from Yorkshire roasters to bakers just down the road, so every cup and plate feels like home. Whether you've just come in from a walk along the harbour or you're settling in with a book, our warm, relaxed space is the kind of place where you can slow down and stay a while.
