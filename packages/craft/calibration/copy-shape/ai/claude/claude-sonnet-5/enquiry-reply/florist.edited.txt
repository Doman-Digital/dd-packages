To put together an accurate quote, a few questions first:

- Is this for a wedding, funeral, or something else?

- Any colours, favourite flowers, or a style/theme you have in mind

Just so you have a general idea of pricing:

- **Weddings** — a simple bridal bouquet usually starts around £45–£65, with buttonholes/corsages from £8 each. Full packages (bouquets, buttonholes, table arrangements, venue flowers) tend to run £400–£1,200+ depending on scale.
- **Funerals** — a simple posy or spray starts from about £45–£60, casket sprays £90–£150, and wreaths from £60 up. We're happy to work around whatever budget you have, so please don't feel you need to spend more than feels right.
