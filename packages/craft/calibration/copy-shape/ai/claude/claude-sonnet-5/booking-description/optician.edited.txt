When you come in for your first visit, we'll start with a thorough eye exam using our latest diagnostic equipment, plus a chat about your vision, lifestyle and anything you're concerned about. One of our optometrists will check the health of your eyes, update your prescription if it's changed, and help you pick out frames and lenses that actually suit you. The whole thing takes about 30–40 minutes, so plan for that — and don't forget to bring along your current glasses or contacts if you wear them.
