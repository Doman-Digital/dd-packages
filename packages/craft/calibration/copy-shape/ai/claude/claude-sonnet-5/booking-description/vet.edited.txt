Your first visit to Hillside Vets is all about getting to know you and your pet, no rushing, no stress. We'll go over your pet's history, give them a thorough nose-to-tail check-up, and chat through anything on your mind, whether that's diet, behaviour, vaccinations, or preventative care. If you have previous medical records, bring them along, and feel free to jot down any questions beforehand so we don't miss anything. Most appointments take around 20–30 minutes.
