Welcome to Riverside Smiles! Think of your first visit as a chance for us to get to know you and your smile — no rushing, just a proper look at your dental health. We'll do a full exam, chat about any concerns or goals you have for your smile, and put together a care plan that actually fits you (or your family). We'll also go over your dental history and leave plenty of time for questions. Just try to get there a few minutes early so you can fill out a quick health form. New to the area or just on the hunt for a dental home you can stick with? Either way, we can't wait to meet you.
