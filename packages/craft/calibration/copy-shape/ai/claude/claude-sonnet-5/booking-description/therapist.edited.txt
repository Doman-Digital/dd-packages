This first session is really just a chance for us to meet and talk about what's brought you to counselling, and to get a feel for whether we're a good fit to work together. It's a relaxed, unhurried conversation, completely confidential, and there's no pressure to book anything further afterwards. We'll also touch on the practical side of things, like how often sessions happen and what they cost, and there's plenty of time for any questions you might have. Sessions are held in person in Brighton.
