This first meeting is your chance to sit down with one of our solicitors, talk through your situation, and get clear, practical advice on the best next steps, whether you're making or updating a will or buying or selling a property. We'll walk you through what to expect in terms of process, timescales, and costs, so there are no surprises along the way. It's a good idea to bring along any relevant paperwork, such as an existing will, property documents, or ID, so we can make the most of the time together.
