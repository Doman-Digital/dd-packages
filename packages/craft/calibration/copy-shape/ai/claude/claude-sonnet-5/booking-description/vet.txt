Your first visit to Hillside Vets is a chance for us to get to know you and your pet in a relaxed, unhurried setting. Our vet will review your pet's history, carry out a full nose-to-tail health check, and talk through any concerns you have, from diet and behaviour to vaccinations and preventative care. Please bring any previous medical records and a list of questions you'd like answered. Appointments typically last 20–30 minutes.
