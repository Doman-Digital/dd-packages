Welcome to Lotus Room! Think of this first session as a chance to get to know each other — we'll chat about your goals, any injuries or health things we should keep in mind, and which style of class might suit you best, whether that's a slower restorative practice or something more dynamic. There's plenty of time for questions too, so nothing feels like a surprise once you join the group.

A few practical notes: try to arrive about 10 minutes early, and wear something comfy to move in. We've got mats and props covered, so don't worry about bringing anything. And if you've never done this before, that's totally fine — no experience needed at all.
