When you come in for your first appointment at Back in Motion, we'll start with a full assessment of your injury or concern, chatting through your medical history and any symptoms you're experiencing. Your physiotherapist will run through any physical tests needed, talk you through what they find, and work with you to put together a treatment plan that suits you. If it's appropriate, we may even get started with treatment there and then. Just a tip: wear or bring along something comfortable that gives easy access to the area we'll be looking at.
