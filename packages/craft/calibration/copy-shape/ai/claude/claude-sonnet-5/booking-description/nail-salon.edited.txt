Welcome to Polished! Think of this first appointment as a chance for us to get to know your nails and your style—we'll chat about shape, length, and finish, and help you figure out whether gel or acrylic suits you best. We'll also take a look at the health of your natural nails and go over how to maintain your set so it stays looking great. Just a heads up: this visit includes a full consultation plus your treatment, so it'll take a bit longer than usual—please plan accordingly!
