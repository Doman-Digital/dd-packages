Thanks so much for getting in touch! We'd genuinely love to help make your tattoo idea happen.

We do custom designs for pretty much anything—whether you've got something specific in mind or you want us to create something totally original. Our artists are pretty versatile too. We can do traditional, realism, geometric, fine line, color work, whatever really works for you.

On pricing, it varies depending on what you're after. The complexity, size, placement, and how detailed you want it all factor in. We charge £150/hour usually, but smaller pieces often get a flat rate quote instead. Custom work tends to run anywhere from £200 to £1000+, though we've definitely done everything from tiny line work to full sleeves.

Here's what'd help us give you a proper quote: tell us what you're thinking? Do you already have something designed, or are you starting from scratch? What size and where are you planning to get it? Once we know a bit more, we can give you a better idea of cost and get you in to chat with one of our artists who'd be a good fit for your style.

If you've got reference images, sketches, anything like that—just send them over. And honestly, if you want to just pop in for a chat, we're always around. Sometimes it's easier to talk it through in person anyway.
