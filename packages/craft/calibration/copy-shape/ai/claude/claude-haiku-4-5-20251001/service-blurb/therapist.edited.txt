I'm a counsellor based in Brighton offering one-to-one sessions for adults. I create a confidential, non-judgmental space where you can talk about what's really going on – whether that's a specific problem you're dealing with or just a sense that something needs to change. Sometimes it helps to have someone listen without trying to fix things or tell you what to do. That's what I'm here for. Together, we can work through what matters to you and figure out what comes next.
