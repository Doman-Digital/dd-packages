At Hillside Vets, we provide compassionate, comprehensive veterinary care for small animals including cats, dogs, rabbits, and other pets. Our experienced team is dedicated to keeping your beloved companions healthy and happy through preventative care, routine check-ups, vaccinations, and treatment of acute and chronic conditions. We understand how much your pets mean to you, and we treat every animal with the same level of care and attention we'd give our own. Located in Kendal, we're here to support your pet's health at every stage of their life.
