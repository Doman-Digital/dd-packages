Your first session with Quiet Harbour Counselling is a chance to meet your counsellor, share what's brought you here, and explore how counselling can support you. We'll discuss what you'd like to work on, your expectations, and how we can work together at a pace that feels right for you. This initial consultation is an opportunity to build trust and establish a foundation for meaningful therapeutic work. If you'd like to book a one-to-one session in Brighton, we're here to listen and help.
