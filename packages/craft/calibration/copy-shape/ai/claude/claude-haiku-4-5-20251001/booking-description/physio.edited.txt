Welcome to Back in Motion! At your first appointment, one of our physiotherapists will chat with you about what's going on—where it hurts, what's limiting you, and your medical history. We'll figure out what's actually causing the problem, then put together a plan that works for your situation. Whether you're bouncing back from an injury, dealing with ongoing pain, or just want to move better, we'll work with you to get you back to doing the things you enjoy.
