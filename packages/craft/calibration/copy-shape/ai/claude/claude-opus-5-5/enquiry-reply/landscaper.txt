Every garden is different, so I can't give you a firm figure without seeing the space. Here are some rough guides for our area to help you plan:

**Patios** (supply and lay, including groundwork, sub-base and pointing)
- Indian sandstone: around £130–£170 per m²
- Porcelain paving: around £160–£220 per m²
- Natural slate or granite: around £170–£240 per m²

A typical 20 m² patio usually ends up somewhere between £2,800 and £4,500. The main things that change the price are:

- **Access:** whether we can get a mini digger in or have to barrow everything through the house.
- **Levels:** how much digging out or building up is needed, and whether the site needs steps or a retaining wall.
- **Drainage:** whether any drainage work is required.
- **Waste:** how much old paving or soil needs taking away.

**Fencing** (supply and install, including removal of the old fence)
- 6ft closeboard with concrete posts and gravel boards: around £110–£150 per metre
- Feather-edge or slatted/contemporary styles: around £140–£200 per metre

Prices depend on ground conditions, slopes, and whether there are old concrete footings to break out.
