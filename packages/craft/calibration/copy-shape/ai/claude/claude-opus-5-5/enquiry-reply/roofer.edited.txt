Thanks for getting in touch. Yes, we can definitely help. Leaks and flat roofs make up most of our work, and we cover Swansea and all the surrounding areas.

It's tricky to give an exact price without seeing the roof, but here's a rough idea of what to expect:

**Leak repairs**
- Smaller jobs, like patching, resealing flashing, or swapping out a few slates or tiles, usually come in at **£150–£400**
- Bigger jobs, such as damaged valleys, lead work, or rotten boards underneath, are more like **£400–£900**

**Flat roof replacement**
Most flat roofs work out at around **£70–£120 per square metre**. It depends on the material you go for and what state the deck underneath is in. To give you a feel for it:
- Garage or small porch: roughly **£1,200–£2,500**
- Single-storey extension: roughly **£2,000–£4,500**

We fit EPDM rubber, GRP fibreglass and torch-on felt, and we're always happy to chat through which one makes most sense for your roof and your budget. Every new flat roof we fit comes with a written guarantee.

Our prices cover labour, materials and getting rid of the old roof. If scaffolding's needed, that's extra, but we'll always tell you upfront so there are no surprises.

For a proper fixed quote, we'll need to come and have a look. Surveys are free and there's no obligation at all. If you could drop us a reply with the following, that'd be a big help:
- Your address or postcode
- Whether it's a leak, a full replacement, or you're not sure yet
- Roughly how big the roof is, if you know
- A few photos, but only if you can take them safely from the ground or a window

And if water's actually coming in right now, just let us know. We'll do our best to get someone out quickly to make it watertight.
