In the kitchen, we clean inside and out of all the cupboards, plus the fridge/freezer, hob, extractor and sink. We clean and descale the bathrooms. Throughout the property we also do the skirting boards, doors, light switches, and the inside of the windows and sills, then vacuum and mop all the floors.

**Extras, if you need them**

- Oven deep clean: £45–£65, depending on size
