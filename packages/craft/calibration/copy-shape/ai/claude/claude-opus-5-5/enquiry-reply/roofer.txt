Thanks for getting in touch. Yes, we can help. Leak repairs and flat roof replacements are most of what we do, and we cover Swansea and the surrounding areas.

It's hard to give an exact price without seeing the roof, but here's a rough guide:

**Leak repairs**
- Small repairs, like a patch, resealing flashing, or replacing a few slates or tiles: usually **£150–£400**
- Larger repairs, such as damaged valleys, lead work, or rotten boards underneath: usually **£400–£900**

**Flat roof replacement**
Most flat roofs cost roughly **£70–£120 per square metre**, depending on the material and the condition of the deck underneath. As a guide:
- Garage or small porch: around **£1,200–£2,500**
- Single-storey extension: around **£2,000–£4,500**

We fit EPDM rubber, GRP fibreglass and torch-on felt, and we're happy to talk through which suits your roof and budget. All new flat roofs come with a written guarantee.

Prices include labour, materials and taking away the old roof. Scaffolding, if needed, is extra, and we'll always tell you upfront.

To give you a firm quote, we'd need to have a look. Surveys are free and there's no obligation. It would help if you could reply with:
- Your address or postcode
- Whether it's a leak, a full replacement, or you're not sure
- Roughly how big the roof is, if you know
- A few photos, if you can take them safely from the ground or a window

If water is coming in now, let us know and we'll try to get someone out quickly to make it watertight.
