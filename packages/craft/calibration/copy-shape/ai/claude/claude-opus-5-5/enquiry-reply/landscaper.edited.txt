I can't give you an exact price without seeing the garden, as every job's a bit different. But here's a rough idea of what things tend to cost round here, so you've got something to plan with.

**Patios** (supplied and laid, including groundwork, sub-base and pointing)
- Indian sandstone: roughly £130–£170 per m²
- Porcelain: roughly £160–£220 per m²
- Natural slate or granite: roughly £170–£240 per m²

For a typical 20 m² patio, you're usually looking at somewhere between £2,800 and £4,500. What pushes it up or down is mostly:

- **Access:** can we get a mini digger round the side, or does it all have to go through the house in wheelbarrows?
- **Levels:** how much we need to dig out or build up, and whether you'll need steps or a retaining wall.
- **Drainage:** whether there's any drainage work to sort out.
- **Waste:** how much old paving or soil needs to go.

**Fencing** (supplied and fitted, and we'll take the old fence away)
- 6ft closeboard with concrete posts and gravel boards: roughly £110–£150 per metre
- Feather-edge or slatted/contemporary styles: roughly £140–£200 per metre

With fencing, it really comes down to the ground: slopes, soil, and whether there are old concrete footings we'll need to break out.
