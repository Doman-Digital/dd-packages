New to Lotus Room? Welcome! Your first session is a relaxed one-to-one in our little Chester studio. We'll start with a chat about you: any yoga you've done before, what you're hoping to get out of it, and any injuries or health things we should know about. Then we'll ease into a gentle introductory practice together.

You don't need any yoga experience at all, and we've got mats and props covered. Just wear something comfy and pop in a few minutes early so you've got time to settle.
