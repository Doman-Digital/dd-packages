Your first lesson with Road Ready is all about getting you settled, whether you're learning in a manual or an automatic here in Derby. We'll have a chat about any driving you've done before, what you're hoping to achieve, and anything you're nervous about. We'll check your provisional licence, then get you behind the wheel and take things at whatever pace feels right for you. By the time we finish, you'll have a clear plan for your lessons and a good idea of what the journey to passing your test looks like.
