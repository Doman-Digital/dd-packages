Your first visit to Ink Well is just a chat. No pressure, no commitment. You'll sit down with one of our artists and talk about the tattoo you've been thinking about. Bring whatever you've got: reference pictures, a rough sketch, or just an idea in your head. We'll work through the design, size, placement and style together until it feels like yours. Got questions about how it all works, looking after it afterwards, or what it'll cost? Ask away. By the time you leave, you should know exactly what you want and feel good about booking in.
