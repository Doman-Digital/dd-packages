Your first appointment at Back in Motion is a thorough assessment with one of our chartered physiotherapists, who will take the time to understand your symptoms, medical history, and goals. We'll carry out a hands-on examination to identify the cause of your pain or movement problem, then explain our findings in plain terms. Where appropriate, treatment can begin in the same session, and you'll leave with a personalised plan to get you moving comfortably again. Please wear loose, comfortable clothing and allow around 45–60 minutes.
