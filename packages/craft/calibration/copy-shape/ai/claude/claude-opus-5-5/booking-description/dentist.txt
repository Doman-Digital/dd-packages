Your first visit to Riverside Smiles is a relaxed, unhurried chance to meet the team and get to know our Taunton practice. We'll carry out a thorough check of your teeth and gums, take any X-rays needed, and talk through your dental history and any concerns you'd like to raise. You'll leave with a clear, personalised care plan and an honest explanation of any treatment options and costs. Patients of all ages are welcome, so feel free to book in the whole family.
