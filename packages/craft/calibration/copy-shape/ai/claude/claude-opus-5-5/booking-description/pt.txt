Your first session at Forge Fitness is a relaxed, no-pressure consultation in our private gym in Sheffield, away from the crowds of a busy commercial space. We'll talk through your goals, training history, and any injuries or concerns, then run through a few simple movement checks to see where you're starting from. By the end, you'll have a clear picture of how we can work together and a plan tailored to you. Just bring comfortable clothes, trainers, and a water bottle.
