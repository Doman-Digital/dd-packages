Your first visit to Riverside Smiles is a chance to meet the team and see our Taunton practice, with no rush. We'll check your teeth and gums properly, take any X-rays you need, and chat about your dental history and anything that's been bothering you. Before you go, we'll give you a clear care plan made for you, along with a straight explanation of your treatment options and what they'll cost. We see patients of all ages, so you're welcome to bring the whole family.
