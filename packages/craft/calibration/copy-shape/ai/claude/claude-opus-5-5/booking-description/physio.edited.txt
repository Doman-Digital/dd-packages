At your first appointment at Back in Motion, you'll sit down with one of our chartered physiotherapists, who'll want to hear all about what's been going on: your symptoms, your medical history, and what you'd like to get back to doing. Then we'll take a hands-on look to work out what's actually causing your pain or stiffness, and talk you through what we find, no jargon. If it makes sense, we can often start treatment there and then, and you'll go home with a plan built around you to get you moving comfortably again. Wear something loose and comfy, and give yourself about 45 minutes to an hour.
