Your first appointment at Petal Row is just a relaxed chat, with no obligation. Whether you're planning a wedding or choosing flowers to say goodbye to someone you love, we'll sit down together and talk through your ideas: the colours you're drawn to, your budget, and any flowers that mean something special to you. We'll show you some of our past work as we go, too.

If you're arranging a funeral, we know time is often short. We'll walk you gently through the choices and look after the details from there, so you don't have to.

There's no need to prepare anything. Just come as you are, and bring along any inspiration or memories you'd like to share.
