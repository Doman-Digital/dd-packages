Your first session with Clear Maths is just a relaxed chat, with no pressure at all. We'll talk about where you're at right now, which topics are giving you trouble, and what you're hoping to get in your GCSE or A level exams. We'll also work through a few questions together, so I can see how you tackle problems and spot the gaps that are worth working on. By the end, you'll have a clear plan for one-to-one tutoring in Reading that fits your exam board, your pace and your goals. Parents are very welcome to come along too.
