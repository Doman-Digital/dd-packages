Before any walks or sits, we'll pop round to your home in Harrogate for a relaxed meet-and-greet. There's no obligation. It's just a chance for your dog to get to know us. We'll have a chat about their routine, personality, feeding and any medical needs, and sort out keys, access and the routes you'd like us to walk. It's also your chance to ask us anything and make sure we're the right fit for your four-legged family member.
