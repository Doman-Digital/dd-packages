Your first visit with Muddy Paws is a relaxed, no-obligation meet-and-greet at your home in Harrogate, giving your dog the chance to get to know us before any walks or sits begin. We'll chat through your pet's routine, temperament, feeding and any medical needs, and agree on keys, access and walking routes. It's also your opportunity to ask questions and make sure we're the right fit for your furry family member.
