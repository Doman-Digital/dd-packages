Come and have a chat with our bakers at Rise. Tell us what you're thinking, whether it's a birthday cake, a weekly sourdough loaf, or something a bit out of the ordinary. We'll have a few things for you to taste, and we're happy to talk flavours, ingredients and any dietary needs. By the end you'll know roughly how long it'll take and what it'll cost. You don't have to order anything. Just bring your ideas, and come hungry.
