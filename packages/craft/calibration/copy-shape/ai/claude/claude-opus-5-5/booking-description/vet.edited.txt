Welcome to Hillside Vets! Your first visit is a relaxed chat with no rush. One of our vets will give your pet a proper nose-to-tail check and get to know them: their history, their routine, and anything you've been wondering or worrying about. We'll cover the basics like vaccinations, flea and worm treatment, diet and, if it's relevant, neutering. Ask us anything you like as we go. If you have records from a previous vet, please bring them along. We can't wait to meet you both!
