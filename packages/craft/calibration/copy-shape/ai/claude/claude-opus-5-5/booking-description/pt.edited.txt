Your first session at Forge Fitness is just a relaxed chat, with no pressure and no hard sell. It happens in our private gym in Sheffield, so you won't be dodging crowds or queuing for kit like you would in a big commercial gym. We'll talk about what you want to achieve, what training you've done before, and any injuries or niggles we should know about. Then we'll go through a few simple movements so we can see where you're starting from. By the end, you'll know exactly how we'd work together, and you'll have a plan built around you. All you need to bring is comfy clothes, trainers and a water bottle.
