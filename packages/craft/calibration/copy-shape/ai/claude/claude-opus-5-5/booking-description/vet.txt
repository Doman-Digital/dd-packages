Welcome to Hillside Vets. Your first appointment is a relaxed, unhurried consultation where one of our vets will give your pet a full nose-to-tail health check and get to know their history, lifestyle and any concerns you have. We'll talk through vaccinations, parasite control, nutrition and neutering where relevant, and answer any questions along the way. Please bring any previous vet records you have, and we look forward to meeting you both.
