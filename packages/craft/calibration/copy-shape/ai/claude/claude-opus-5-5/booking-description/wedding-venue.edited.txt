Why not come and see Holloway Barn for yourselves? Our converted barn sits in the Shropshire countryside just outside Ludlow, and a first visit is nice and relaxed. We'll show you round the barn, courtyard and grounds, have a chat about your ideas, guest numbers and the dates you have in mind, and answer any questions about how a day here actually works. It usually takes about an hour, and you're very welcome to bring along anyone whose opinion matters to you.
