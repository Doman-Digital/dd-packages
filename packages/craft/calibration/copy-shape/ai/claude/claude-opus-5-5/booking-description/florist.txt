Your first appointment is a relaxed, no-obligation chat with our team at Petal Row, whether you're planning a wedding or arranging flowers to say goodbye to someone you love. We'll talk through your ideas, colours, budget and any flowers that hold special meaning, and show you examples of our work along the way. For funerals, we understand time is often short, so we'll guide you gently through the choices and take care of the details from there. Come as you are. You don't need anything prepared, just bring whatever inspiration or memories you'd like to share.
