Your first session is just a relaxed chat, with no pressure at all. It's a chance to tell me a bit about what's brought you to counselling and what you're hoping to get out of it. We can talk about how we might work together, and you're welcome to ask me anything, whether that's about how counselling works, confidentiality, or practical things like times and fees. You don't have to commit to anything afterwards. It's really just about helping you work out whether Quiet Harbour Counselling feels right for you.
