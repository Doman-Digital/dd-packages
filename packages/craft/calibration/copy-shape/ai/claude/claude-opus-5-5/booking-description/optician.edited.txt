Your first visit to Clearview Opticians is nothing to worry about. You'll sit down with one of our qualified optometrists at our independent practice in Bury St Edmunds, and there's no rush. We'll start with a chat about your eyes, your day-to-day life and anything that's been bothering you. Then we'll do a thorough eye exam, and we'll explain what we're doing at each step. It helps if you bring your current glasses or contact lenses, plus a list of any medications you're taking. Allow about 45 minutes.
