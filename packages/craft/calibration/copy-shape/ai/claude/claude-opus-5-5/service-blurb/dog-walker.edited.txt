At Muddy Paws, we walk dogs and look after pets all over Harrogate, so you can get on with your day (or your holiday) knowing they're in good hands. Maybe your dog prefers a quiet one-to-one walk, or loves nothing more than charging around the Stray with their mates. Maybe you've got a cat or a rabbit who'd rather stay at home with a friendly visitor. Either way, we'll fit around their routine, not the other way round. We're fully insured, lots of local families already trust us, and we honestly treat every pet as if they were our own. Expect muddy paws and very happy tails.
