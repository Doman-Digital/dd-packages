Every morning at Rise, we bake our sourdough in small batches. We give it a long, slow rise and use flour from British mills, and that's what gets you a crackly crust and a deep, tangy crumb. Next to the bread there's always something new on the counter: cakes, bakes and whatever's in season, all made by hand in our kitchen in Hebden Bridge. Come in early if you want first pick, or order ahead if you've got a birthday or something else to celebrate.
