Lotus Room is a small yoga studio tucked into the middle of Chester, and our classes are for everyone, whatever your body, whatever your experience. Maybe you've never done yoga before, or maybe you've been practising for years and want to go deeper. Either way, our teachers will take you through movement, breathing and relaxation at a pace that feels right for you. We keep our classes small on purpose, so you'll get real attention from your teacher, in a calm, friendly room where you can properly switch off and come back to yourself.
