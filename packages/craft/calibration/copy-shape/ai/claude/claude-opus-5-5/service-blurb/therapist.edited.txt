At Quiet Harbour Counselling, I offer one-to-one counselling for adults in Brighton: a calm, confidential space to talk about whatever's on your mind. Maybe you're dealing with anxiety, low mood, relationship troubles or grief. Or maybe you just feel stuck and can't quite say why. Either way, we'll go at your pace and focus on what matters to you. Together, we can make sense of things, help you feel steadier, and find a way forward that feels right.
