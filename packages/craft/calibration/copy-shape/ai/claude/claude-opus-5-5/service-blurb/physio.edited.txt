At Back in Motion, we're a team of experienced physiotherapists here in Stevenage, and our goal is simple: to help you recover from injury, ease your pain and get back to doing the things you love. We start every treatment by properly getting to know you and your body, so we understand exactly what's going on. From there, we'll put together a plan that's built around you, mixing hands-on therapy, exercises that actually target the problem, and practical advice you can use day to day. Whether it's a sports injury, a stubborn back or neck, or you're finding your feet after surgery, we'll be with you every step of the way until you're moving freely again.
