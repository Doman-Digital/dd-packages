You'll find The Copper Kettle just off Whitby's harbourside, a proper neighbourhood cafe where the coffee's freshly brewed, the tea's loose-leaf, and the food is hearty and homemade, from breakfast right through to afternoon tea. We make everything ourselves, from the scones still warm from the oven to whatever soup's on today, and we buy our ingredients from Yorkshire suppliers just down the road. So whether you've been battered by the wind on the beach or you're after a long natter with friends, come on in. The kettle's always on.
