At Hillside Vets in Kendal, we care for cats, dogs, rabbits and all sorts of other small pets. Whether your pet needs a routine vaccination, a check-up or neutering, or something more involved like diagnostics, surgery or dental work, our experienced team is here for them from their first visit right through to their golden years. We'll always take the time to listen, talk you through your options in plain terms, and look after your pet as if they were one of our own.
