An eye test with us is about much more than checking whether you need new glasses. We take our time looking at the overall health of your eyes, checking for early signs of things like glaucoma, cataracts and macular degeneration, and we'll talk you through what we find as we go. We're an independent practice right in the centre of Bury St Edmunds, so we never rush your appointment, and any advice we give is based on your eyes and the way you live.
