At Rise, we bake small batches of naturally leavened sourdough every morning, using long, slow fermentation and flour from British mills for a loaf with a crackling crust and a deep, tangy crumb. Alongside our bread, you'll find a changing counter of cakes, bakes and seasonal treats, all made by hand in our Hebden Bridge kitchen. Pop in early for the pick of the day's bake, or order ahead for celebrations and special occasions.
