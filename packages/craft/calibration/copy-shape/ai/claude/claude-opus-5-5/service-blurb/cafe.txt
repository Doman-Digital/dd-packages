Tucked just off Whitby's harbourside, The Copper Kettle is a friendly neighbourhood cafe serving freshly brewed coffee, loose-leaf teas and hearty homemade food from breakfast through to afternoon tea. Everything from our warm scones to our daily soups is made in-house using ingredients from local Yorkshire suppliers. Whether you're stopping by after a blustery walk on the beach or settling in for a catch-up with friends, there's always a warm welcome and a fresh pot on the go.
