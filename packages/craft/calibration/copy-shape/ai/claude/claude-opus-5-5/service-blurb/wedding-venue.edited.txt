Holloway Barn sits in the Shropshire hills just outside Ludlow. It's an old barn we've restored with a lot of care, and on your wedding day it's yours alone. There are oak beams overhead, lime-washed walls, and views across the countryside from almost every window. It's rustic without being rough around the edges. It works just as well for a small ceremony with your closest people as for a full party of 150. Our team will be with you from the first time you come to look around until the last song of the night, sorting out the details so you can relax and enjoy the day.
