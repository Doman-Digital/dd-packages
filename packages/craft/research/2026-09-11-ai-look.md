# Research: the AI-built look, 2026-09-11

**Research, not rules.** The rules are the tell catalogue in
[`CHARACTER.md`](../CHARACTER.md). Licences are in
[`src/direction/licences.ts`](../src/direction/licences.ts). This file keeps
the evidence behind both, and says how far each piece can be trusted.

Three Perplexity passes on 2026-09-11, with the claims that mattered then
checked by hand. It was first written into `dd-packages/design-rules.md`
(commit `e4f4c06`) before anyone knew craft existed. What it found that craft
lacked was taken into craft on 2026-09-24.

## How far to trust each claim

| Tier | What it means | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | The licensor's own page, the repo's own file, the person who made the decision | A rule, a licence entry |
| Named person | A dated, attributable post by a real practitioner | A reason in a `why` |
| Aggregator | Listicles and "state of AI slop" sites with no method | A lead to check, never on its own |

Several sources the first pass leaned on (sailop.com, overlayqa.com,
aiskill.market, downloadicons.com, medo.dev) read as programmatic SEO
content. Every claim below that rests only on them is marked.

## Why the look exists

- **Indigo. Primary.** Adam Wathan, who made Tailwind, on X, 2025-08-07: "I'd
  like to formally apologize for making every button in Tailwind UI
  `bg-indigo-500` five years ago, leading to every AI generated UI on earth
  also being indigo."
  (x.com/adamwathan/status/1953510802159219096). Tailwind UI's demos were
  copied into public code; models learned "interface means indigo" from it.
  In craft: `ai-violet`, `blue-purple-gradient`.
- **Geist. Unconfirmed.** The research said shadcn/ui's
  `tailwind.config.cjs` sets Geist as the default sans. When checked by hand
  the repo had moved to a monorepo and that file was not at the cited path. The
  closed loop (Vercel ships Geist, the shadcn docs and v0) is plausible and the
  rule holds without it, because Geist is on the reflex list for being picked,
  not for its origin. In craft: `reflex-font-2`.
- **Lucide. Well corroborated, not primary.** The default icon set across
  v0, Bolt, Lovable and the shadcn registry; Replit mixes in Heroicons. No
  shadcn document found that says so outright. In craft: the icon-tile tells,
  which judge the tinted tile, not the library.
- **"73% of AI frontends use Inter". Aggregator only.** No method. Do not
  quote it.

## Type

**Checked, OFL:** Velvetyne's Basteleur, Gulax and Le Murmure (velvetyne.fr).
Free, no seat count, any number of client domains with the licence notice kept.
They are in the licence register as facts. They are not a house shortlist: a
face used because it is on a list is the agency's reflex font.

**Foundries, as read on their own pages on 2026-09-24:**

- **Blaze Type.** Its `/license` page says one licence covers every domain.
  Its EULA sells a professional licence for 1 to 10 websites, and lets an
  agency buy in a client's name at the listed price. The EULA is the contract:
  buy per client.
- **Power Type.** Research reported a Super License of 10 self-hosted
  domains. The EULA did not load when checked. Unverified.
- **Grilli Type.** Licences cover domains the licensee controls, priced by
  monthly visitors, so a client site needs the client's own. Unlimited tiers
  start at $10,000 per style. Ruled out on cost.
- **Tunera.** Exists and distributes OFL work; no face names could be
  confirmed. Browse it directly before citing any.

**Still open:** a face for trades, finance and B2B that reads as precise
without being the Inter reflex, and one for luxury and hospitality. Every site
decides its own from its own world. The prompt below finds candidates to check,
not answers:

```
Recommend 3 or 4 SIL OFL typefaces for UK trades and finance client sites:
precise and legible, not display faces. Avoid Inter, Roboto, Open Sans,
Poppins, Montserrat, DM Sans, Manrope, Geist and Space Grotesk. Separately,
3 or 4 OFL typefaces for luxury and hospitality sites. For each, name the
foundry, link the foundry's own download page, and confirm the licence on the
foundry's own licence page, not a third party.
```

## Icons

**Checked, MIT:** Phosphor (six weights), Tabler (one stroke grid), Iconoir
(the smallest of the three). **Checked, IconScout Simple License:** Unicons.
**IconScout premium** (Student Developer Pack, 60 icons a month for a year):
each licence covers a single end product, and never a tool or template. See
the register for what that rules out.

Phosphor was picked here as a house icon base. Craft does not take that up:
one set on every client site is the sameness the estate signal measures.
