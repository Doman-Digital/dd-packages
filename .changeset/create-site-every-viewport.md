`measure.mjs` uses craft's every-viewport measure (DOM-787, part of DOM-772): one measure that the portal gates use too. It measures every page at 320, 375, 390, 430, landscape 844 x 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920, phone first, with a width sweep from 320 to 1920, and keeps its earlier failures (a page that does not answer, sideways scroll, no viewport meta, text under `measure.minFontPx`, targets under 44 x 44, fields under 16px, labels, noindex on a preview). New failures: text over text and a cut-off title at any width; on a short phone, the main action (`measure.mainAction`) off the first screen, fixed layers over a quarter of the screen, oversized or stretched images, wrong keyboards and missing autocomplete, phone numbers that are not `tel:` links; in landscape, fixed layers over half the height; at 1440 and 1920, body lines over 80 characters. `site.programme.json` `measure` gains `mainAction`, `thresholds`, `sweep` and `baseline` (known failures in craft's baseline format, written by `--update-baseline`); a failure names the widths it happens at. New sites install `@domandigital/craft` ^0.20.0, which carries the measure.
