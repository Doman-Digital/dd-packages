Two new review-tier copy tells (catalogue 2026.10.2). `sales-tagline` reports advert lines such as "so your budget goes where it counts", "real results" and "pages that convert". `outcome-promise` reports a hoped-for result written as if delivered: "we'll turn that into enquiries", "once the first areas are working", "competitors you can outdo", "exactly what convinces". Ordinary statements of the work ("we'll rewrite your rewiring page", "once the fuse board is fitted") pass. Both print for review and never block, following the house rule that a new tell starts in review.
