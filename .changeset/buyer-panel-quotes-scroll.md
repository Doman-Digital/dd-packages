Taps and typing now match text whatever its quotes, spacing or case, so a buyer who types "What's included" reaches a button named "What’s included", and "Your child's age" reaches a field labelled "Your child’s age". Tapping the name of an option in a dropdown on screen now chooses it. Scrolling moves the scrollable box under the screen when the window itself cannot scroll, and the screen reading reports that box's position.
