New package: the signed synthetic-submission protocol (X-DD-Synth v1) for client form handlers and dd-checks. Sign and verify with a required replay-guard hook and two-key rotation, a recipient lock, turnstileConfigCheck, fire-and-forget sendBeacon and sendPurgeReceipt, a Sentry and log scrubber, and published test vectors. Web Crypto only; tested in Node and in workerd.
