`craft copy claims <paths>` lists every sentence holding a price, figure, date or named source, marked sourced or UNSOURCED, for a person to check against the primary source. `findClaims` and `formatClaims` do the same in code. No tell can tell a true figure from a false one; this makes the checking a list instead of a hunt.
