Decision confidence: reviewing a proposed change, with attribution to Eunice Ordor and the provenance of the principle (a phone-layout review on 15 October 2026) in CHARACTER.md and PRINCIPLES.md. New `craft direction review --notes design-review.json` weighs feedback against the visitor's job in `art-direction.json`. Each note keeps the observation, the visitor problem under it, the proposed solution, the evidence and the smallest defensible change apart, and judges the suggestion and the recommendation separately. The problem is observed, inferred, preference or unknown: only something inspected (a screenshot, rendered behaviour, source, a measurement, the page's content) makes it observed, a report from an audit, stakeholder or customer makes it inferred, and a claim at a width nobody looked at stays unknown. A recommendation is a confirmed correction, a design hypothesis, an optional refinement or no change, and a review can end on "no change is recommended". Changes climb a ladder from copy to restructure; a structural step or an addition (carousel, script, dependency, animation, testimonial, call to action, repeated content) has to say why every smaller step was not enough. A second carousel is not supported where a gallery exists, information is not removed without an observed cost, a reasoned section order is not overridden by a preference, repetition and similarity to other sites are not defects without a stated cost to the visitor, and a change that needs a fact nobody supplied is held rather than written in. `craft direction validate` also raises an inferred identity finding when a job that worries about who the visitor would deal with first meets a person after three or more sections, and asks for a name and a line, not a biography moved up or a portrait. New `differentiation` lens, `design-review.schema.json`, and exports `assessReview`, `isStructural` and the review types. Fixtures: a calibration review of a plumbing homepage plus clinic and salon reviews, which reach different outcomes for different reasons.
