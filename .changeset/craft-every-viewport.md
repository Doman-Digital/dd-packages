Every viewport, and a phone optimised beyond fit (DOM-787, part of DOM-772). `@domandigital/craft/phone` gains `measureViewports(page, options)`, `measureSite(browser, options)` and a `craft viewports` command beside `craft phone-first`. They take a page through the ladder (320, 375, 390, 430, landscape 844 x 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440, 1920, phone first in order and in every report) and a width sweep from 320 to 1920 in 16 to 32px steps, resizing the one loaded page in place. At any width: sideways scroll, visible text over other visible text and a cut-off page title, with the exact widths that fail. On a short phone (375 x 667): the main action (`mainAction`, a selector, global or per route) on the first screen or pinned; fixed and sticky layers over a quarter of the screen; images more than 2.5 times their displayed width or narrower than it; phone, email and number fields with the wrong `type` or `inputmode`; personal-data fields with no `autocomplete`; UK phone numbers in the text that are not `tel:` links. On a landscape phone: header and fixed layers over half the height. At 1440 and 1920: body lines over 80 characters and stretched images. Every threshold has a house default (`DEFAULT_THRESHOLDS`) and can be overridden. The new checks are baselined like the 375px ones: they use the same `phone-first.baseline.json` and `craft phone-first --measured`, and `measurePhone` and the existing baseline format work as before. STANDARD.md section 10 gains "Optimised for the phone" and "Every viewport is intended".
