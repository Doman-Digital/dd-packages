Decision confidence. A page in `art-direction.json` can now record `confidence`: the widths its decisions were examined at (`mobile`, `tablet`, `desktop`) and the evidence it offers (`proof`: work, people, credentials, reviews, experience or outcomes, each with a section, `first-look` or `on-request` depth, and a reason tied to the customer's job). `craft direction validate` reads it and prints findings tagged observed, inferred or unknown, each with the lens it belongs to (relevance, hierarchy, evidence, identity, action, disclosure, mobile) and why it matters to that visitor. There is no score and no conversion claim, and nothing is required: a file without `confidence` validates and reports exactly as before, and no evidence kind, founder portrait, slideshow, phone number or section order is ever demanded. A finding made on one width is never carried to another; widths nobody examined are reported as unknown. `craft direction research` asks where the visitor looks, what would settle it and what the next step is, without assuming a phone or a portrait. Exports `assessConfidence`, `validateConfidence`, `viewportOf` and the new types; the JSON Schema gains `confidence`. CHARACTER.md, PRINCIPLES.md, STANDARD.md section 10 and the README describe it, and three fixtures (sole-trader plumber, private physiotherapy practice, appointment-based salon) show one principle giving three different answers.
