The house copy standard moves into craft. `COPY.md` ships in the package, `craft copy --gate` applies the house policy (the blocking tier fails the run), and `craft tells list --json` publishes each copy tell's house tier so `copy-check` reads the policy instead of keeping its own list. Catalogue 2026.09.4 adds the density tier (`phrase-density`, `aphorism-density`, `contraction-scarcity`, `sentence-rhythm`, `repeated-sentence`, `heading-shape`, `heading-echo`) and four review tells from research (`ing-tail`, `vague-attribution`, `closing-summary`, `false-range`), all warn. The emoji tell now catches sparkles, the green tick box and other emoji in the older symbol blocks.
